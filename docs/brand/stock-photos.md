## What the next shoot must replace

The Operator's own shoot replaces this file, and a record is kept for every
photograph that replaces a stock one. The brief already asked for: warm natural
light, a family together or a well-kept Lokasi, Indonesian people in modest
dress, service shown. Never a jenazah, a coffin close-up, digging or crying, and
never another company's photograph — the same guardrails the guide sets.
