# The Operator collects every payment and pays partners out itself

Every payment on makam.co.id, including a Lokasi Mitra's Petak Makam and Layanan tariffs, lands in YIEM's single Xendit account; YIEM is the seller of record and disburses each Lokasi Mitra amount per order once it is paid and the work is confirmed done. The payment research recommended a gateway split (a Xendit xenPlatform sub-account per Lokasi Mitra, the partner as seller); we chose central collection instead so partners need no gateway KYC or monthly sub-account fee, refunds are decided in one place before money leaves YIEM, and YIEM's income can be a platform fee on top of the partner's tariff. Legal exposure of holding partner funds was deliberately left out of scope (see `.scratch/makam-v1/issues/13-payment-licensing.md`).

**Amended 2026-09-28, second clause, corrected rather than merely noted.** The sentence above originally read "disburses each Lokasi Mitra **and Mitra Jasa** amount". That was **true for a Lokasi Mitra and false for a Mitra Jasa**: a Potongan carries a non-null `lokasiId`, the platform fee reaches a Potongan only through `trigger.ts` and only via `lokasi.lokasiId`, a Pencairan item is filtered by `eq(pencairanItem.lokasiId, …)`, and no caller of `catatItemLayananMitraJasa` exists outside a test. `src/app/staf/mitra-jasa/pencairan/page.tsx:6` says "not built yet". The word **and Mitra Jasa** is therefore struck from the decision as written, because an ADR that claims a capability the code lacks is worse than one that is silent — a reader relies on it. The **intent** is unchanged and remains the design: central collection is what makes disbursing a Mitra Jasa possible, and the three payments carrying it are `partner_share` on the order, a Pencairan item, and a Potongan. What is missing today is the wiring, and it is tracked as **ticket 50 first** (51 and 56 are both blocked by it), then 56. Note also that a `Pekerjaan` finished and unpaid currently produces **no signal at all** — no tick, no Antrean row, no alert — which is a gap in itself and not only a missing payout path.


**Amended 2026-09-25** ("Tech stack for a solo engineer with AI agents"): the single collecting account is SumoPod, not Xendit. Central collection stands; because SumoPod has no payout API, YIEM pays partners out by manual bank transfer, recorded with proof, until a second gateway is added after v1 launch.

**Amended 2026-09-25** ("Operator entity: PT Jaya Korpora Prima and YIEM's role"): the collector and seller of record is the Operator, PT Jaya Korpora Prima, not YIEM, which has left the product. Every "YIEM" above now means the Operator; the decision itself is unchanged.

**Amended 2026-09-28** (owner, and the first amendment that touches how the Operator *earns* rather than who collects). Central collection is what creates the Biaya Layanan Platform's role in the first place, so the two are decided together: **the fee exists because the Operator is the seller of record and disburses a partner itself.** A TPU order has no partner tariff to sit on and no partner to disburse, so **no Biaya Layanan Platform ever appears on a TPU order** — the Operator's income there is the Biaya Pengurusan plus the **Margin Layanan TPU**, which lives *inside* the price the family pays a TPU and is never shown as a line of its own. This is a deliberate asymmetry with a Lokasi Mitra order, not an omission, and it is recorded because the code reached it quietly: the rule traces to a builder's hand-off note in ticket 12 and an AC in ticket 43, with **no owner decision recorded anywhere** until now. Two things remain open and are not settled by this amendment: the **rate** at which the Margin Layanan TPU is set, and the fact that **no payment path to a Mitra Jasa exists at all** — `payouts` is a placeholder and `partner_share` is nowhere in the tree, so the margin is currently income on paper that cannot reach the person doing the work. Related glossary: `Margin Layanan TPU`, `Biaya Layanan Platform`.
