## 1. Order of events

### What the law says (de jure): the IPTM comes first

- Pasal 16: "Pemakaman jenazah … dilakukan dalam waktu kurang dari 24 (dua puluh empat) jam **setelah memperoleh izin penggunaan tanah makam**". [P3/2007, primary]
- Pasal 17(1) says that for every burial the ahli waris or penanggung jawab "wajib memperoleh izin" from the Kepala SKPD, attaching the Lurah's death report, the RS/Puskesmas letter, the KK and the KTP. Pasal 19 says the SKPD then issues the IPTM on the basis of that report. [primary]
- Pasal 32: every use of tanah makam requires an IPTM. [primary]
- The detailed procedure is delegated to a Pergub (Pasal 21, 34). **I did not find that Pergub.** [gap, same as the earlier research]

Read literally, the Perda puts the permit before the burial, with the burial within 24 hours of it.

### What actually happens (de facto): burial first, IPTM filed afterwards

Every practical description found has the same order. The TPU office handles the burial on the spot. The TPU's **surat pengantar** for the IPTM is issued **after the burial**. The IPTM itself follows at PTSP Kelurahan, now filed through JakEVO.

- PTSP Kelurahan Lenteng Agung, describing its own procedure (2016). For a new plot: *"keluarga almarhum dapat memakamkan langsung ke TPU dengan berkas persyaratan… Semua berkas dibawa ke TPU. **Setelah jenazah Almarhum dimakamkan, Keluarga almarhum akan memperoleh Surat Pengantar dari TPU**, untuk selanjutnya semua berkas dibawa ke PTSP Kelurahan terdekat untuk pengurusan penerbitan SKRD dan Surat ijin makam Baru."* Tumpang works the same way: "Semua berkas ditunjukkan ke TPU. Setelah jenazah Almarhum dimakamkan, Keluarga almarhum akan memperoleh Surat Pengantar Rekomendasi dari TPU…". For **perpanjangan**, by contrast, the ahli waris goes to the TPU first. [PTSP-LA]
- Kepala UP PTSP Kecamatan Tanah Abang (2019): *"Selama ini untuk mengurus IPTM harus ada surat pengantar dari TPU, lalu dibawa ke kelurahan, dan setelah IPTM diterbitkan dibawa lagi ke TPU."* A "PTSP Goes to TPU" counter at Karet Bivak (Mon–Thu 10.00–16.00) was opened so families would not have to shuttle between the offices. [BJ-2019, official-quoted]
- The current checklist requires "Surat Pengantar dari TPU (Asli)" for baru, perpanjangan and tumpangan alike. [IPTM-check, primary] The checklist also takes a Kelurahan death report and an RS death letter, which a family rarely has in hand before a same-day Muslim burial. That supports the post-burial order, though this is my inference.
- A 2024 explainer gives a different order: RT/RW → kelurahan → TPU (pick plot) → PTSP → IPTM → bury. It quotes no official and still describes retribusi payment, which ended in 2024. I treat it as a generic description, not how things happen. [B1-2024, secondary]
- In 2026 Distamhut tells ahli waris to deal *"secara langsung melalui loket resmi di tempat pemakaman umum (TPU) setempat dan layanan perizinan di PTSP kelurahan"*. That gives two separate touchpoints: the TPU loket for the burial and PTSP for the permit. [Antara-2026, official-quoted]

**Conclusion (confidence medium–high):**

1. The family (or RT/RW, or the rumah duka) contacts the **TPU office (Satpel/pengelola)** first. The TPU checks the documents, allocates a petak (or confirms the family plot for a tumpang), and digs and buries the same day.
2. After the burial the TPU issues the **surat pengantar**.
3. The IPTM is then filed through JakEVO or at PTSP Kelurahan. It is issued within 1 working day.

So in practice the IPTM follows the burial, even though Pasal 16 literally says the reverse. **[unverified: I found no 2025–26 official text that states the post-burial order outright. The strongest evidence is the 2016 PTSP-LA blog and the 2019 PTSP quote.]**

A tumpang follows the same order. The TPU verifies that the plot's IPTM is still active and that ≥3 years have passed since the last burial (Pasal 36(5)), buries, then issues a "surat pengantar tumpang". The tumpang IPTM needs a scan of the existing IPTM. [PTSP-LA; IPTM-check] PTSP-LA also states a cap of **no more than 4 tumpang bodies per plot**. [PTSP-LA, semi-official, 2016; not found in the Perda, **unverified** as a current rule]

## 2. Who allocates the plot, choice of block and TPU, and full TPUs

- **Choosing the TPU:** the family chooses, subject to capacity. In 2016 the Kadis said *"pemohon bisa bebas memilih sesuai ketersediaan lahan"* and told residents to check availability online before going to the kelurahan. [BJ-2016, official-quoted]
- **The specific petak** is assigned by the TPU office. Every explainer describes "datang ke TPU dan pilih petak makam" or "menentukan petak bersama petugas". Karet Bivak's Kepala TPU (2014) said blocks AA1/AA2/A1/A2 are "ditentukan berdasarkan lokasi" (the front block is AA1). [Detik-2014; B1-2024] The family can pick among the block classes that still have free petak. The TPU picks the exact blad and number. **[unverified in any regulation. Since the 2024 Rp 0 tariff, block class no longer affects price, so there is no longer any reason to prefer or avoid a class on cost.]** A.III (formerly the free block for the poor or unidentified) still exists in listings.
- **Online availability:** Distamhut's [ketersediaan-perpetakan](https://distamhut.jakarta.go.id/ketersediaan-perpetakan) lists, per TPU → religious unit → Blok (e.g. AAII) → Blad, the count of free petak (e.g. "Blad021 122 PETAK"), the price "Rp.0", and **named staff with phone/WhatsApp numbers** plus the TPU office phone. It is information only, with no booking. [Avail, primary] The portal also points to pertamananpemakaman.jakarta.go.id/v140/t203 for "real-time" availability. [Portal]
- **When TPUs are full:** in Oct 2025, **69 of 80 TPUs were full and "hanya menerima pelayanan makam tumpang"**. About 118,348 petak remained across 11 TPUs: Rawa Terate, Cipayung, Cilangkap, Bambu Apus, Cipinang Besar (Jaktim), Rorotan (Jakut), Tanah Kusir, Srengseng Sawah, Kampung Kandang (Jaksel), Tegal Alur, Pengadungan (Jakbar). At about 100 burials a day, that is roughly 3 years of capacity. [Detik-2025a, official-quoted] For Jaksel families without a family plot, the options are **tumpang** or a **new burial at another TPU that still has petak**. *"Kebijakan teknis diserahkan kepada pengelola TPU."* [Detik-2025b, official-quoted] No new land was budgeted in APBD-P 2026. [NU-2026]
- Product implication: for most families, a "new plot at the TPU of choice" is not available. The real choices are a tumpang in a family plot (which needs an active IPTM), or a new plot at one of about 11 outer TPUs.

## 3. Timing

- **Death to burial:** the Perda requires burial within 24 hours of the permit. Burial after **more than 24 hours** needs an *izin penundaan waktu pemakaman*, up to 5 days from death and extendable, except for infectious disease. The body must then be in a standard peti. [P3/2007 Pasal 16, 22, primary] For Muslims, same-day burial (often the morning after a night death) is the religious norm. That is common knowledge; **[unverified: no Jakarta statistic found]**.
- **Burial hours:** *"Waktu memakamkan dan memindahkan, serta mengabukan atau kremasi jenazah, dilakukan antara pukul 06.00 WIB sampai dengan pukul 18.00 WIB, kecuali apabila Kepala SKPD … mengizinkan"* outside those hours. [P3/2007 Pasal 30, primary] **Night burial is therefore exceptional and needs SKPD permission.** One community site says Kramat Jati TPU operates 08.00–15.00 with after-hours access only in emergencies. [rw010bonpal.com, **unverified**]
- **Weekends and holidays:** TPUs do bury on weekends; burial is a daily service and Distamhut says the average is about 100 a day (Detik-2025a). Sudin Tamhut Jaksel keeps TPU services running over Lebaran. A search snippet of the Pemkot Jaksel article says TPU Jeruk Purut administration ran **1×24 jam** during cuti bersama, including IPTM perpanjangan and tumpang, with 37 staff on duty. [selatan.jakarta.go.id, **unverified: page blocked, snippet only**]
- **Surat pengantar outside office hours:** no official source found. The per-TPU staff phone and WhatsApp numbers on the Distamhut availability page, with several staff per TPU "for different shifts", suggest families reach the TPU out of hours by phone. Because the surat pengantar is issued after the burial, it is **not on the critical path** for a same-day burial. **[unverified]**

## 4. JakEVO and PTSP

- **SLA:** "Waktu Penyelesaian: **1 Hari Kerja**". The retribusi is "-" (Rp 0). The issuing authority is **UP PMPTSP Kelurahan**. The application is filed by web. [IPTM-check] The service page shows "Durasi: 1 Hari". Its 10-step mechanism still includes an SKRD step, a leftover from the retribusi era. [IPTM-page]
- **Hours:** PTSP Kelurahan counters are open **Mon–Fri 08.00–16.00** (e.g. Kel. Utan Panjang, Kel. Senen). [pusat.jakarta.go.id kelurahan pages via search, **snippet-level**] JakEVO accepts submissions online at any time. Since the SLA is counted in working days, **a weekend or holiday submission is processed from the next working day**. **[unverified: I could not open JakEVO. The "hari kerja" basis is from the official checklist.]**
- **Proxy (kuasa):** allowed. The checklist asks for *"Scan Asli Surat kuasa di atas kertas bermaterai … dan KTP-el orang yang diberi kuasa"*. [IPTM-check, primary] Nothing limits who the kuasa holder may be, so a YIEM staff member holding a signed surat kuasa bermeterai (current meterai Rp 10.000) can file. The applicant or penanggung jawab is still the ahli waris, whose KTP and KK are required. [IPTM-check] The Perda allows "ahli waris **atau penanggung jawab pemakaman**" to apply (Pasal 32(2)). Whether a yayasan itself can be the *penanggung jawab* rather than a kuasa holder is **[unverified]**.
- Distamhut also promotes filing through **JAKI** (the Jakarta Kini app) as well as JakEVO, to reduce face-to-face contact that invites pungli. [WK-2026, official-quoted]

## 5. Grace window after burial, pungli, and "jasa urus"

- **No grace window for a new IPTM is written anywhere I could find.** The Perda has none for a first IPTM; the 3-month rule in Pasal 33(2) applies only to *perpanjangan*. The IPTM's 3-year term runs from the burial: "IPTM berlaku 3 tahun sejak pemakaman". [B1-2024, secondary] Using tanah makam without an IPTM is an offence under Pasal 32 and 49(1), but I found no report of enforcement against late filers. **[unverified: how long families actually take, and whether a TPU withholds anything until the IPTM is shown. The 2019 PTSP quote says the IPTM is "dibawa lagi ke TPU", i.e. registered back at the TPU.]**
- **Fees:** everything is Rp 0 for DKI-KTP residents. That covers the IPTM (baru, perpanjangan, tumpang), digging and closing by PJLP staff, a 3×3 tent, chairs, sound system, hearse and pemulasaraan. It is funded by the APBD at 82 TPUs. The Kadis asked the public **not to tip** field staff, including PJLP. [WK-2026; Antara-2026, official-quoted]
- **Pungli (June 2026):** the Kadis Distamhut admitted pungli persists. The internal kind is "pelan-pelan kita tertibkan". A **"pola baru"** involves people outside the TPU, including oknum RT/RW, taking money from families who think *"itu orang pemakaman"*. Distamhut later said it did not accuse RT/RW as institutions. It asks families to go **directly** to the official TPU loket and PTSP, notes that official staff wear uniforms and ID and may not accept cash, and asks people to **report "pihak atau oknum yang bertindak sebagai perantara pengurusan pemakaman dengan meminta biaya di luar ketentuan"**. Complaints go to JAKI, the TPU posko, or hotline 0816-878-889 / 0858-9000-9132. [Kompas-2026; Antara-2026, official-quoted]
- **What this means for YIEM (interpretation):** Distamhut's public message is "urus sendiri, jangan lewat perantara". A yayasan charging to handle a service the Pemda says is free and simple will be seen as a *perantara*, the very category Distamhut asks citizens to report, unless the following hold:
  - it holds the Perda 3/2007 Pasal 10 izin operasional and has reported its tariffs to the SKPD (see the earlier research, §2.5);
  - its fee is presented clearly as a service fee for a service Pemda gives free;
  - it never collects money "for" the TPU or PTSP;
  - it offers the free DIY path alongside.

  Commercial "jasa urus pemakaman 24 jam" operators already exist (e.g. kamboja.co.id, which charges extra for night and holiday service), so the market practice is real. [search snippet, **unverified**]

## 6. Deceased who died outside Jakarta, or who hold a non-DKI KTP

- **Eligibility** (Pasal 3): TPUs serve (a) DKI residents who die **inside or outside** Jakarta, and (b) *other* people who die **inside** Jakarta. [P3/2007, primary] **A non-DKI-KTP person who died outside Jakarta is therefore not eligible.** The Penjelasan exempts DKI Pemprov employees, pensioners, officials and current or former DPRD members who live outside DKI. [P3/2007 Penjelasan Pasal 3]
- **Documents for a body "dari luar daerah"** (Pasal 17(2)):
  - an RS/Puskesmas examination letter *from the place of origin*;
  - a death report from the **Lurah/Kepala Desa of origin**;
  - a **surat pengantar kematian from the origin region's health SKPD** (Dinas Kesehatan);
  - KK and KTP.

  The earlier research described this as "a surat pengantar from the SKPD". It is specifically the *origin* region's health office. [primary] A body from abroad needs the hospital letter, a letter from the Indonesian embassy, a letter from Kemlu, and the passport (Pasal 17(3)). A body with no identity documents needs only the Lurah's letter and the RS/Puskesmas letter (Penjelasan Pasal 17(1)(c)).
- **Fees for non-DKI KTP:** the 2026 "gratis" statements are framed as "gratis … **bagi warga ber-KTP Jakarta**". [Antara-2026] The retribusi was abolished outright by Perda 1/2024, so no legal basis remains to charge non-residents. Whether TPUs treat them differently in practice is **[unverified]**.
- A body leaving Jakarta for burial elsewhere must be reported to the SKPD with the Dinas Kesehatan letter (Pasal 18). The transport permit is issued under Pasal 19.

## Summary answers

1. **Order.** In practice: TPU office → petak allocated → same-day burial → TPU surat pengantar → IPTM via JakEVO or PTSP (1 working day). The Perda (Pasal 16) literally requires the IPTM before burial, but the practice evidence (PTSP Lenteng Agung, PTSP Tanah Abang) puts the IPTM afterwards.
2. **Plot.** The family picks the TPU and block class if free petak remain. The TPU staff assign the blad and number. Distamhut publishes free-petak counts plus staff phone numbers, with no booking. 69 of 80 TPUs take tumpang only. The alternative is a new plot at about 11 outer TPUs.
3. **Timing.** Burial within 24 hours (a penundaan permit allows up to 5 days). Burial hours are 06.00–18.00 unless the SKPD allows otherwise. TPUs work weekends and holidays. Out-of-hours surat pengantar: [unverified], but it is not needed before the burial.
4. **JakEVO/PTSP.** 1 working day, Rp 0. Counters are open Mon–Fri 08–16. Weekend submissions are processed on the next working day [unverified]. A kuasa (surat kuasa bermeterai + KTP of the holder) is explicitly allowed.
5. **Grace window.** None written for a new IPTM. Pungli is admitted by Distamhut (June 2026), including outside intermediaries. Distamhut tells families to avoid perantara, which is a positioning risk for YIEM.
6. **Outside Jakarta.** Non-DKI KTP and died outside DKI: not eligible (Pasal 3). DKI resident who died outside: Pasal 17(2) documents, including the origin region's Dinkes surat pengantar.
