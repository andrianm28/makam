## 1. National framework (PP 9/1987)

- There are three kinds of cemetery. **Tempat Pemakaman Umum (TPU)** is open to everyone regardless of religion and is managed by the Pemda Tingkat II or the Pemerintah Desa. **Tempat Pemakaman Bukan Umum (TPBU)** is managed by a *badan sosial dan/atau badan keagamaan*. **Tempat Pemakaman Khusus** is a cemetery with historical or cultural meaning. [PP9 Pasal 1]
- Land status: TPU land holds **Hak Pakai** for as long as it is used for burial. TPBU land holds Hak Pakai, except wakaf land used for burial, which is **Hak Milik**. [PP9 Pasal 3]
- The maximum plot size is **2.5 m × 1.5 m**, with a minimum depth of 1.5 m. This applies to both TPU and TPBU. [PP9 Pasal 4(3)]
- A TPBU is managed by a social and/or religious body or legal entity. It needs a permit from the Bupati/Walikota (in Jakarta, the Gubernur), issued after approval from the Mendagri. [PP9 Pasal 5(3)-(4)]
- In a TPU, the Pemda "mengusahakan agar tidak memberatkan warga masyarakat". A TPBU is **"tidak dibenarkan dikelola secara komersial"**. [PP9 Pasal 6(3)] The Pemda may close a TPBU that deviates from the rules. [PP9 Pasal 8(3)] A TPU may charge retribusi "dengan tarif yang wajar". [PP9 Pasal 8(4)]
- PP 9/1987 **says nothing about tenure length, renewal, tumpang, or pre-need purchase**. Those are left to Perda. [PP9, whole text read]
- Commercial private cemeteries do operate. The UGM thesis studies San Diego Hills as a case of a commercial TPBU and the gap between it and PP 9/1987. [UGM] **[unverified: I did not find the licensing instrument (Perda Karawang / izin) San Diego Hills or Al-Azhar actually operate under]**

## 2. Jakarta (DKI)

### 2.1 Izin Penggunaan Tanah Makam (IPTM)

- Every use of tanah makam in a taman pemakaman requires an IPTM from the head of the SKPD responsible for cemeteries. The ahli waris or penanggung jawab pemakaman applies in writing. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 32]
- **An IPTM is valid for 3 years and "dapat diperpanjang setiap 3 (tiga) tahun."** [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 33(1)]
- To extend, the ahli waris or penanggung jawab applies in writing **"paling lama 3 (tiga) bulan setelah masa izin penggunaan tanah makam berakhir."** [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 33(2)]
  - The later tariff regulation used a different grace period. An extension could be filed **up to 3 years** after the lease ended, and a plot not extended after that could be "digunakan untuk pemakaman ulang". [DKI-3/2012 Lampiran E.a.3.c] That Perda was revoked by DKI-1/2024 (see 2.4), so the 3-month rule in Perda 3/2007 is the one left standing. **[unverified: how TPU staff enforce the grace period in practice]**
- Plots are reused in rotation: "Setiap petak tanah makam ... harus digunakan untuk pemakaman dengan cara bergilir atau berulang pada tiap berakhirnya penggunaan tanah makam." [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 36(1)]
- Consequence of letting it lapse, per the official portal: **"Makam tanpa IPTM yang aktif dapat ditumpang dengan jenazah lain tanpa memerlukan persetujuan ahli waris pengguna lahan sebelumnya."** Jakarta TPU occupancy is ">95%, bahkan hampir 100%". [DKI-portal]
- Transitional rule: IPTMs issued under the old Perda 2/1992 stay valid until they expire. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 50]
- Using tanah makam without an IPTM (Pasal 32) is an offence carrying up to 3 months' kurungan or a fine of up to Rp 50 juta. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 49(1)]

### 2.2 Tumpang (layered burial)

- Every TPU plot is to be used for tumpang burials unless the ground is technically unsuitable (waterlogged). [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 36(2) + Penjelasan]
- Tumpang is done among family members. **For a non-family member, written permission is required from the ahli waris or the party responsible for the plot.** [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 36(3)] The official portal's line quoted above describes the exception: once the IPTM has lapsed, that consent is no longer needed. [DKI-portal]
- The new body may go above or beside the old one, at least 1 m below the surface. [Pasal 36(4)] Tumpang is allowed only **≥3 years after the previous burial**. [Pasal 36(5)]
- A tumpang IPTM has no 3-year term of its own. Its validity **"tergantung masa berlaku IPTM sebelumnya"**, so it rides on the plot's existing permit. [DKI-IPTM]

### 2.3 Buying in advance

- **Not allowed.** "Petak tanah makam hanya diperuntukkan bagi jenazah atau kerangka dan tidak diperbolehkan untuk pesanan persediaan bagi orang yang belum meninggal dunia." [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 37]
- TPUs serve DKI residents who die inside or outside Jakarta, and other people who die inside Jakarta. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 3]

### 2.4 Tariffs (retribusi)

- Historical rates under Perda 3/2012, Lampiran E, for a 3-year sewa tanah makam: blok **AA.I Rp 100.000**, **AA.II Rp 80.000**, **A.I Rp 60.000**, **A.II Rp 40.000**, **A.III Rp 0** (A.III is for abandoned bodies and families who cannot pay; Penjelasan Pasal 88). Tumpang cost 25% of the base rate. An extension cost 50% or 100% of the base rate (the Lampiran text reads "tiga tahun pertama" twice). [DKI-3/2012]
- **Today it is free.** Perda 1/2024 lists the Retribusi Jasa Umum objects as kesehatan, kebersihan, parkir tepi jalan, pasar, and pengendalian lalu lintas. Pemakaman is not on the list. [DKI-1/2024 Pasal 67(1)] The same Perda revokes Perda 3/2012. [DKI-1/2024 Pasal 116] The official IPTM checklist shows "Biaya Retribusi: –". [DKI-IPTM] A Pemkot official confirmed that the IPTM is no longer charged but must still be renewed every 3 years through PTSP. [Kompas-2024; DKI-portal] Distamhut's availability list shows "Rp.0". [DKI-avail]
- **[unverified]** The underlying national reason is presumably that UU 1/2022 (HKPD) dropped pemakaman from retribusi jasa umum. I did not check the text of the UU.
- The brief asked about a Pergub on tariffs. I found **no Pergub on cemetery tariffs**. Tariffs sat in a Perda (3/2012) and are now zero. I also did not find the Pergub implementing Perda 3/2007 on IPTM procedure (Pasal 21/34 delegate to a Pergub). **[gap]**

### 2.5 Yayasan offering cemetery services in DKI (directly relevant to YIEM)

- An "usaha pelayanan pemakaman" run by the public **must take the form of a yayasan** and hold an **izin operasional** from the SKPD. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 10(2)] The Penjelasan defines the yayasan as one ratified by Kemenkumham and "semata-mata tidak mencari keuntungan". The izin must be re-registered every 3 years. [Pasal 10(4)] Administrative activity may not take place inside the TPU grounds. [Pasal 10(5)] **The yayasan's tariffs must be reported to the SKPD.** [Pasal 10(6)] The usaha is subject to retribusi. [Pasal 10(7)]
- The scope of "usaha pelayanan pemakaman" is: pengurusan jenazah, angkutan, peti, perawatan jenazah, rumah duka, kremasi, penyimpanan abu, and "kegiatan atau usaha lain di bidang pelayanan pemakaman". [Pasal 9]
- Families may not build permanent structures on a plot or put up anything other than a plakat. Only ornamental plants approved by the SKPD are allowed. [Pasal 42] Every plot must carry a plakat makam. [Pasal 35(2)] Running an unlicensed service can bring up to 6 months' kurungan or a fine of Rp 50 juta. [Pasal 49(2)]
- The Gubernur may cooperate with a yayasan in running cemetery services. [Pasal 44]

### 2.6 Documents required (Jakarta)

For a burial (Perda), when the person died in Jakarta: surat keterangan laporan kematian from the **Lurah**; surat keterangan pemeriksaan jenazah from a hospital or Puskesmas; a copy of the **KK**; a copy of the deceased's **KTP**. [DKI-3/2007 Pasal 17(1)] There are separate lists for deaths outside Jakarta (which add a surat pengantar from the SKPD) and for deaths abroad. [Pasal 17(2)-(3)]

For the IPTM, per the current official checklist [DKI-IPTM]:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Electronic form (via JakEVO) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 2 | Applicant/penanggung jawab KTP-el + KK (WNA: KITAS/visa/paspor) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 3 | Surat kuasa bermaterai + KTP of the kuasa holder (if applying by proxy) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 4 | KTP + KK of the deceased | ✓ | | ✓ |

**A letter from the RT/RW is not on the Jakarta list.** The Kelurahan death letter serves that purpose. (Bandung does ask for RT/RW; see 3.2.)

## 3. Other big cities

### 3.1 Surabaya (Perda 4/2025)

- Burial in a Pemda-run TPU needs "persetujuan dari Dinas". The details are delegated to a Perwali. [SBY-4/2025 Pasal 17]
- Tumpang is allowed because space is limited. It happens on a family member's plot, or with a written *pernyataan tidak keberatan* from the ahli waris if the person is not family. It requires 1 m of cover and **≥3 years since the previous burial**. [Pasal 18]
- **Booking a plot before death is prohibited:** "melakukan pemesanan petak makam sebelum ada jenazah". Building kijing and fencing plots are also prohibited. [Pasal 24]
- The plot is 1.25 m × 2.5 m. [Pasal 13] The Dinas's service is limited to digging the grave and the mound. [Pasal 12(5)]
- Old-style "kepemilikan lahan makam", proven by a purchase receipt, stays valid, but must be reported to the Dinas **within 2 years** of the Perda or it lapses. [Pasal 31]
- The Pemda may take over abandoned cemeteries and plots. [Pasal 11(2)]
- Some old TPUs are run by RT/RW/LPMK. They must get the Pemda's approval of their tariffs and may not refuse residents holding a Surabaya KTP. [Pasal 5, 8, 9]
- Third parties offering cemetery services (supplies, grave cleaning, "bentuk pelayanan pemakaman lainnya") on Pemda cemetery land **need a Walikota permit**, delegated to the Dinas. [Pasal 22(2),(4),(5)] Cooperation with a badan usaha or ormas in managing a cemetery is possible. [Pasal 23]
- **The Perda itself sets no tenure term and no renewal.** Those are left to the Perwali, which is due within 6 months. Existing burial permits stay valid until they expire. [Pasal 32-33] **[unverified: the Perwali text and any current Surabaya term or fee. One search snippet cited Rp 100.000 per 3 years for new cemeteries, but I could not trace it to an enacted text.]**
- For the rumah duka or crematorium, the documents are a death certificate from the RS/doctor or an akta kematian, a surat kuasa if the applicant is not an heir, the deceased's KTP, and the applicant's KTP. [Pasal 21(3)]

### 3.2 Bandung (Perda 5/2023)

- Burial requires **online registration** through the city's cemetery information system, rolled out in stages. [BDG-5/2023 Pasal 8, 9(2)-(3)] That system is SIMPELMAN, which offers "Pemakaman Baru dan Tumpang", "Pembayaran her-registrasi atau perpanjangan sewa lahan makam", and hearse hire. [SIMPELMAN]
- Documents: a **surat keterangan laporan kematian from the RT/RW** and/or from a hospital or Puskesmas; the heir's KTP and/or a copy of the KK; a copy of the deceased's KTP. [Pasal 9(1)]
- **Tumpang: "tidak harus satu rumpun keluarga, tidak harus sama jenis kelaminnya dan tidak mengharuskan adanya izin dari keluarga/ahli waris"**. It requires 1 m of cover and must wait until the previous body permits. Non-Muslims may be exempted. [Pasal 11] This is much looser than Jakarta or Surabaya.
- **No reserved plots:** "tidak diperbolehkan untuk Tanah Makam Cadangan bagi orang yang belum meninggal dunia". Existing reservations were recognised for 2 years only. [Pasal 12]
- Plot size: Muslim plots 2 × 1 m; non-Muslim plots 2.5 × 1.5 m. [Pasal 13] Permanent structures are prohibited and existing ones are to be dismantled in stages. [Pasal 23, 27]
- Perda 5/2023 does not set a tenure term. It revokes the service parts of Perda 19/2011 jo. 3/2017 and keeps their implementing rules until the PDRD Perda (5 Jan 2024). [Pasal 29-30] The earlier retribusi Perda 3/2017 defined an **IPPM (Izin Penggunaan Petak Makam)** priced **per year**: land provision Rp 25.000/m²/year, perpanjangan Rp 20.000/year, tumpang Rp 75.000 then Rp 30.000/year. [BDG-3/2017 **[unverified copy via pasal.id]**] **[unverified: Bandung's current renewal period and fee after its 2024 PDRD Perda; SIMPELMAN still offers "perpanjangan sewa"]**

### 3.3 Pattern across cities

All three cities ban reserving a TPU plot before death. All three allow tumpang after about 3 years with 1 m of cover. Consent for tumpang varies: Jakarta and Surabaya require consent from a non-family ahli waris, while Bandung requires none. Tenure varies too: Jakarta has 3-year renewable IPTMs, Bandung historically charged per year, and Surabaya leaves it to a Perwali. Fees are trending to zero or near zero, while the obligation to renew and register remains. [sources above]

## 6. Answers to the ticket's questions

1. **Rules for the right to use a plot.** PP 9/1987 only covers land status, plot size and who may manage a cemetery. Tenure is set by each region.
   - Jakarta: a 3-year IPTM, renewable every 3 years, applied for within 3 months after expiry, now free, via JakEVO. A lapsed plot can be tumpang'd by strangers without the family's consent.
   - Tumpang is allowed ≥3 years after the last burial with 1 m of cover, and a non-family tumpang needs the ahli waris's written consent while the IPTM is active.
2. **Other cities.** In Surabaya, tenure is delegated to a Perwali, pre-booking is banned, and tumpang needs non-family consent. In Bandung, online registration is mandatory, tumpang needs no family consent, reserved plots are banned, and historically the tariffs ran per year.
3. **Private cemeteries.** They sell perpetual, one-payment usage rights with bundled maintenance, sold pre-need. The right is transferable before use with operator approval. This is agent-sourced and not verified against operator terms. Commercial operation sits uneasily with PP 9/1987 Pasal 6(3).
4. **Documents.** A death certificate from the RS/Puskesmas, a death report letter from the Lurah (Jakarta) or RT/RW (Bandung), and the KTP + KK of the deceased and of the applicant. A surat pengantar from the TPU is needed in Jakarta. A surat kuasa is needed if applying by proxy. For Perpanjangan: the applicant's KTP/KK, the TPU letter and the previous IPTM.
5. **Buying in advance in a TPU.** **No.** It is explicitly banned in Jakarta (Pasal 37), Surabaya (Pasal 24d) and Bandung (Pasal 12). It is allowed only in private cemeteries.

## 7. Implications for the Petak Makam lifecycle and Perpanjangan (interpretation, not cited fact)

- **Pemesanan Terencana cannot target TPU plots.** Pemesanan Terencana must be limited to Lokasi Makam that are TPBU, private or wakaf, and whose own rules permit reservation. TPU Lokasi can only take Pemesanan Saat Duka. The Lokasi Makam model needs a flag such as `jenis` (TPU / TPBU / wakaf / private) or `boleh_pesan_terencana`.
- **Tenure is a property of the Lokasi, not global.** Model `masa_berlaku` per Lokasi Makam: 3 years for DKI TPUs, possibly 1 year in Bandung, perpetual (null) for private or wakaf. Model the Perpanjangan grace window per Lokasi as well: 3 months in Jakarta per Perda 3/2007.
- **Suggested lifecycle states:** `tersedia → terisi (aktif, berlaku s/d X) → masa berlaku habis (grace) → tidak aktif / dapat ditumpang`.
  - Perpanjangan returns a plot to `aktif` for another term.
  - Tumpang is a new burial in an existing Petak Makam, so one Petak holds many Almarhum. In Jakarta the tumpang permit shares the existing IPTM's expiry, so the **term belongs to the Petak (or IPTM), not to each Almarhum**.
  - Tumpang eligibility needs the last burial date (≥3 years) and a consent flag when the new body is not family.
  - "Dikosongkan" is really "open to tumpang by others", not an empty plot.
- **In DKI TPUs the platform cannot grant tenure; only the Pemda (via JakEVO/PTSP) issues the IPTM.** A Perpanjangan on makam.co.id for a DKI TPU is therefore a **facilitation service**: the platform collects documents and files them with JakEVO, possibly as a surat kuasa holder, since the checklist accepts kuasa. The deliverable is the new IPTM, recorded against the Petak. The government charge is Rp 0, so any price is YIEM's service fee and must be presented that way to stay "harga transparan".
- **The Perpanjangan document set is small and known**: the Pemegang Hak's KTP + KK, the previous IPTM (original), the surat pengantar from the TPU, and a surat kuasa if YIEM files. The previous IPTM is the natural proof that someone is the Pemegang Hak (relevant to ticket 07). The "Pemegang Hak" in regulation is the *ahli waris / penanggung jawab* named on the IPTM.
- **Reminder value is real.** In Jakarta, lapsing lets strangers be buried in the plot without the family's consent. Expiry reminders and an "IPTM expiring" dashboard are a strong, low-risk feature, though the notification channel is still open.
- **YIEM selling Layanan in DKI TPUs likely needs a Perda 3/2007 Pasal 10 izin operasional** (as a yayasan, re-registered every 3 years, tariffs reported to the SKPD, no admin work on TPU grounds). Surabaya likewise requires a Walikota permit for grave-cleaning and similar services on Pemda cemetery land. This belongs under money flow and trust. Also, headstone Layanan must respect local bans on permanent structures and kijing (DKI, Bandung, Surabaya), so a TPU headstone Layanan may be limited to a plakat.
- **Pemesanan Terencana contract terms (map's "not yet specified")** can borrow the private-cemetery pattern: transfer allowed only before use, with operator approval and an admin fee. For wakaf land, commercial resale may conflict with wakaf rules (ticket 03) and PP 9/1987 Pasal 6(3).
