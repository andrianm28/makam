# Research: Regulation of cemetery plots and tenure

Ticket: `../issues/04-cemetery-plot-regulation.md`
Researched: 2026-09-24
Confidence markers: **[primary]** = read in the regulation text or on an official government or operator page; **[secondary]** = news or an authorised sales-agent site; **[unverified]** = inferred or not traced to a source.

## TL;DR for the domain model

- **Public TPU (Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung): the right to use a plot is a time-limited permit, not ownership.** In Jakarta this is the *Izin Penggunaan Tanah Makam* (IPTM). It lasts 3 years and can be extended in further 3-year periods. If it is not extended, the Petak Makam can be reused for a *tumpangan* (layered) burial without the heirs' consent.
- **Buying a TPU plot in advance is prohibited.** Jakarta (Perda 3/2007 Pasal 37), Surabaya (Perda 4/2025 Pasal 24 huruf d) and Bandung (Perda 5/2023 Pasal 12) all ban reserving a plot for someone still alive. **This means a Pemesanan Terencana is only legal at private or wakaf cemeteries, not at a Pemda TPU.**
- **Private memorial parks** (San Diego Hills, Al-Azhar Memorial Garden) sell a right that the operator describes as permanent, with no extension fee and no maintenance dues. The operator, not BPN, issues the certificate. The right can be transferred (hibah, sale, inheritance) for an admin fee.
- **Pemegang Hak in law = "ahli waris atau penanggung jawab"**, i.e. the heir or the person responsible for the burial. In Jakarta, only they may apply for the IPTM and its extension (Pasal 32–33).
- **Documents for a TPU burial (Jakarta):** a death report letter from the Lurah, a death examination letter from a hospital or Puskesmas, and copies of the deceased's KK and KTP (Pasal 17). The online service also asks for the applicant's KTP and KK, plus the previous IPTM for an extension or tumpangan. Bandung explicitly accepts a death letter from the RT/RW.

## 1. National layer: PP 9/1987

Source: PP No. 9 Tahun 1987 tentang Penyediaan dan Penggunaan Tanah untuk Keperluan Tempat Pemakaman, https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Details/64044/pp-no-9-tahun-1987 (PDF: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/54259/PP%20NO%209%20TH%201987.pdf) **[primary]**

- **Three cemetery types (Pasal 1):**
  - *Tempat Pemakaman Umum* (TPU) is run by the Pemda Tingkat II or the desa.
  - *Tempat Pemakaman Bukan Umum* is run by a social and/or religious body. The Penjelasan also calls it "Tempat Pemakaman Partikelir … pengelolaannya dilakukan oleh swasta".
  - *Tempat Pemakaman Khusus* covers historic sites.
- **Land status (Pasal 3):**
  - TPU land has *Hak Pakai* for as long as it is used as a cemetery.
  - Non-public cemetery land has *Hak Pakai* "yang dapat diperpanjang" (Penjelasan).
  - Wakaf cemetery land has *Hak Milik*, "karena fungsi wakaf pada dasarnya bersifat kekal".
- **Plot size (Pasal 4(3)):** at most 2.5 m × 1.5 m, at least 1.5 m deep. Under the Penjelasan, one plot may hold more than one body of the same family.
- **Who may run a non-public cemetery (Pasal 5(3)–(4)):** only a *Badan/Badan Hukum* of a social or religious nature, never an individual. It needs a licence from the Bupati/Walikota (in Jakarta, the Gubernur) after approval from the Mendagri.
- **Pasal 6(3):** a TPU may charge "ringan dan wajar" retribusi. A non-public cemetery "tidak dibenarkan dikelola secara komersial".
  - **[unverified]** This is at odds with the actual commercial model of San Diego Hills and Al-Azhar Memorial Garden. Their legal structuring, for example a yayasan paired with a PT as seen at Al-Azhar, likely exists to satisfy this rule. I did not trace their licences.
- **Implementing rule:** Kepmendagri 26/1989 (pedoman pelaksanaan PP 9/1987) exists but was not read. **[unverified content]**

## 2. Jakarta: Perda DKI 3/2007 tentang Pemakaman

Source PDF (Pemprov DKI PTSP): https://pelayanan.jakarta.go.id/download/regulasi/peraturan-daerah-nomor-3-tahun-2007-tentang-pemakaman.pdf
Also on JDIH DKI (https://jdih.jakarta.go.id/dokumen/detail/13437) and BPK (https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/302478/perda-prov-dki-jakarta-no-3-tahun-2007). Status: berlaku. It replaced Perda 2/1992. **[primary]**

### 2.1 Who may be buried
- **Pasal 2(2):** "taman pemakaman" covers Pemda-owned cemeteries and non-Pemda ones, "antara lain Tanah Wakaf".
- **Pasal 3:** eligible are Jakarta residents who die inside or outside Jakarta, plus other people who die inside Jakarta.

### 2.2 IPTM: the right to use a plot
- **Pasal 16:** a burial must take place within 24 hours *after* the IPTM has been obtained.
- **Pasal 32(1):** every use of cemetery land requires an IPTM from the Kepala SKPD bidang pemakaman, today the Dinas Pertamanan dan Hutan Kota.
- **Pasal 32(2):** the applicant is the "ahli waris atau penanggung jawab pemakaman", applying in writing.
- **Pasal 33(1):** "Izin penggunaan tanah makam … berlaku untuk jangka waktu 3 (tiga) tahun dan dapat diperpanjang setiap 3 (tiga) tahun."
  - **No cap on the number of extensions** appears in the Perda.
- **Pasal 33(2):** the extension is requested in writing by the "ahli waris atau penanggung jawab atas penggunaan tanah makam", "paling lama 3 (tiga) bulan setelah masa izin … berakhir". This is a 3-month grace window *after* expiry.
- **Pasal 34:** details are delegated to a Pergub.
  - **[unverified]** Search results name Pergub 17/2014 as the implementing regulation, but I did not open it.
- **Pasal 49(1):** using cemetery land without an IPTM (a breach of Pasal 32) is a criminal violation, punishable by up to 3 months' kurungan or a fine of up to Rp 50 juta.

### 2.3 Consequence of not extending
- The official Pemprov page https://www.jakarta.go.id/page/layanan-pemakaman-di-jakarta states: "Makam tanpa IPTM yang aktif dapat ditumpang dengan jenazah lain tanpa memerlukan persetujuan ahli waris pengguna lahan sebelumnya." **[primary]**
- This follows from Pasal 36(1): every plot "harus digunakan untuk pemakaman dengan cara bergilir atau berulang pada tiap berakhirnya penggunaan tanah makam".

### 2.4 Tumpangan (layered burial), Pasal 36
1. Plots are used in rotation when a permit ends.
2. Every plot is to be used for tumpangan unless the ground does not allow it. The Penjelasan gives the example of waterlogged soil.
3. Tumpangan is done among family members. For a non-family member, the plot's ahli waris or responsible party must give **written permission**, but only while the IPTM is active (see 2.3).
4. The new body goes above or beside the old one, with at least 1 m of soil above the body.
5. Tumpangan is allowed only after the earlier body has been buried for at least 3 years.
- The PTSP fee table **[primary, but pre-2024 rates]** priced tumpangan at 25% of the block rate.

### 2.5 Advance purchase, Pasal 37
- The text: "Petak tanah makam hanya diperuntukkan bagi jenazah atau kerangka dan **tidak diperbolehkan untuk pesanan persediaan bagi orang yang belum meninggal dunia.**"
- This is a flat ban on pre-need reservation in Jakarta TPU.

### 2.6 Other plot rules
- **Pasal 35:** plots are at most 2.5 × 1.5 m, at least 1.5 m deep, and must carry a plakat nisan.
- **Pasal 42:** permanent structures, fences or objects that separate graves, non-degradable coffins and trees are prohibited. Only ornamental plants chosen by the SKPD are allowed.
- **Pasal 26:** moving a body to another plot needs a written permit, and the body must have been buried for at least 1 year.

### 2.7 Burial documents, Pasal 17(1): death inside Jakarta
The ahli waris or responsible party attaches:
- a. surat keterangan laporan kematian dari **Lurah** setempat;
- b. surat keterangan pemeriksaan jenazah dari rumah sakit atau Puskesmas;
- c. fotokopi **KK**;
- d. fotokopi **KTP** of the deceased.

For a death outside Jakarta, Pasal 17(2) adds a letter from the Lurah or Kepala Desa of the place of death and a *surat pengantar* from the SKPD. For a death abroad, Pasal 17(3) requires a letter from the embassy and one from the Kemlu.

**RT/RW letter:** the Perda does not mention it. In practice, the Lurah's death report is normally issued on a pengantar from the RT/RW. **[unverified practice]**

### 2.8 Current IPTM service (2024 onward)
- **Online application:** via Jakevo (jakevo.jakarta.go.id) or the PTSP offices. One service covers "Makam Baru/Perpanjangan/Tumpangan". The PTSP page (https://pelayanan.jakarta.go.id/site/detailperizinan/163) states a service duration of **1 hari**. **[primary]**
- **Requirements on jakarta.go.id** **[primary]**:
  - scan of the applicant's KTP-el and KK (KITAS or passport for foreigners);
  - scan of the deceased's KTP and KK;
  - a death certificate from a Puskesmas or RS;
  - the death report from the kelurahan;
  - the previous IPTM: the original for an extension, a scan for a tumpangan;
  - a surat kuasa plus the proxy's KTP when filing on someone's behalf.
- **Fee:**
  - Since **Perda DKI 1/2024** on Pajak dan Retribusi Daerah, there is **no retribusi** for the IPTM, according to the jakarta.go.id page and Kasudin Pertamanan Jakpus as reported by Kompas (https://www.kompas.com/cekfakta/read/2024/08/21/131546082/...). **[primary + secondary]**
  - The extension is still mandatory every 3 years.
  - **[unverified]** I did not read Perda 1/2024 itself to confirm the exemption clause.
- **Old fee table, for history** (per 3 years, PTSP page https://pelayanan.jakarta.go.id/site/detailperizinan/165):
  - Blok AA.I: Rp 100.000
  - AA.II: Rp 80.000
  - A.I: Rp 60.000
  - A.II: Rp 40.000
  - A.III: Rp 0
  - That page also asked for a letter from the TPU management and an heir statement on meterai.

## 3. Other large cities

### Surabaya: Perda Kota Surabaya 4/2025 tentang Pengelolaan Pemakaman dan Pengabuan Jenazah
https://jdih.surabaya.go.id/uploads/peraturan/2025perda3578004.pdf **[primary]**. It replaces Perda 13/2003.

- **Pasal 17:** a burial in a Pemda TPU needs "persetujuan dari Dinas". Details go to a Perwali.
- **Pasal 18 (tumpang):**
  - allowed because space is limited;
  - on a family member's plot, or on another plot with a written *pernyataan tidak keberatan* from that family;
  - at least 1 m of soil above the body;
  - at least 3 years after the earlier burial.
- **Pasal 24 huruf d:** it is prohibited to make a "pemesanan petak makam sebelum ada jenazah".
- **Pasal 24 huruf b:** new kijing or buildings on plots are prohibited.
- **Pasal 9 huruf b:** RT/RW/LPMK managers may not refuse holders of a Surabaya KTP.
  - Surabaya TPU are partly managed by the RT/RW/LPMK, a notable structural difference from Jakarta.
- **Pasal 11(2):** the Pemda may take over abandoned plots (*petak makam yang terlantar*).
- **Pasal 32:** permits issued before this Perda stay valid until they expire.
- **Tenure length is not stated in the Perda itself.**
  - **[secondary/unverified]** Search snippets of an older Surabaya retribusi regulation (jdih.surabaya.go.id/uploads/peraturan/perda_56.pdf) mention Rp 100.000 per body for 3 years at new cemeteries. Not opened.

### Bandung: Perda Kota Bandung 5/2023 tentang Pelayanan Pemakaman Umum
https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/283653/perda-kota-bandung-no-5-tahun-2023 **[primary]**. The BPK PDF is a scanned image, so I read the article text through the mirror at pasal.id **[secondary mirror of primary]**.

- **Pasal 9(1):** the documents are:
  - a. surat keterangan laporan kematian from the **RT/RW** and/or a hospital or Puskesmas;
  - b. the heir's KTP and/or a copy of the KK;
  - c. a copy of the deceased's KTP.
- **Pasal 9(2):** the documents are uploaded online through the city's system (SIMPELMAN, https://diciptabintar.bandung.go.id/simpelman/, which also handles "perpanjangan sewa lahan makam").
- **Pasal 11 (tumpang):**
  - any TPU plot may be layered;
  - **no family relationship, same gender or heir permission is needed**, which is looser than Jakarta;
  - at least 1 m of soil;
  - allowed once the earlier body "memungkinkan";
  - non-Muslims may be exempted.
- **Pasal 12:**
  - (1) plots are only for a body or skeleton, and "**tidak diperbolehkan untuk Tanah Makam Cadangan bagi orang yang belum meninggal dunia**";
  - (2) existing reserved plots were recognised for only 2 years after enactment.
- **Tenure length and fees:** these came from Perda 19/2011 as amended by Perda 3/2017. News reports put the rate at Rp 25.000/m² for one year and Rp 20.000 per year to extend. Pasal 29–30 carry these rules over until the new PDRD Perda. **[secondary/unverified]** News reports say Bandung waived burial fees for residents holding a Bandung KTP from 2024.

### Kota Tangerang
- Disperkim Kota Tangerang (2021): "setiap penggunaan tanah makam di tanah pemakaman umum dapat diperpanjang dalam jangka waktu 3 (tiga) tahun". Applications go online at perizinanonline.tangerangkota.go.id. https://disperkim.tangerangkota.go.id/berita/perpanjangan-ijin-penggunaan-tanah-makam **[primary]**

**Pattern:** the 3-year renewable permit, tumpang after 3 years with at least 1 m of soil, and the ban on advance reservation are consistent across Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung and Tangerang. The details differ in heir consent for tumpang, fees, online system, and whether the document chain starts at the RT/RW or the Lurah.

## 4. Private memorial parks

Neither operator publishes contract terms on a first-party page I could reach. The sandiegohills.co.id site has no FAQ or terms. Most results come from **authorised sales-agent sites**, which are **[secondary]**. The binding terms are in the purchase agreement (PPJB/"agreement"), which I did not see.

### San Diego Hills (Karawang; Lippo Karawaci group)
- **Right sold** (agent FAQ https://sales-sandiegohills.com/faq/): "hak untuk memakamkan jenazah manusia pada sebidang tanah". It is marketed as perpetual, with **no extension fee and no monthly or annual fee**.
- **Certificate:** issued by San Diego Hills, not BPN, within 1 month of full payment. Agent sites call it a "Sertifikat Kolektif Makam". **[secondary]**
- **Transfer:** allowed for inheritance, sale or gift, via balik nama at the SDH office (Jakarta or Karawang). The fee is **Rp 5.000.000 per certificate from 1 Oct 2025**. **[secondary]**
- **Capacity:** up to 3 skeletons per grave in the case of transfers (pemindahan). **[secondary]**
- **Pre-need:** plots are sold pre-need, with installments. One agent's T&C mentions a pre-need programme for ages 18–64 and cancellation on 2 consecutive missed payments (https://salessandiegohills.com/panduan/ketentuan-dan-syarat-pemesanan-lahan-makam). Funeral-service fees are not prepaid; an insurance product ("Future Care") covers them instead. **[secondary]**
- **Burial documents:** the agent FAQ lists the death certificate, the deceased's KTP and KK, and the KTP and KK of the person handling the burial. **[secondary]**

### Al-Azhar Memorial Garden (Karawang)
- **Operator:** a cooperation between Yayasan Pesantren Islam Al Azhar (YPIA) and PT Nuansa Usaha Mandiri. **[secondary, stated on official-agency site https://www.makamalazhar.co.id/pertanyaan-umum/, "Agency Resmi … No. 06392"]**
- **Right sold:** a "sertifikat pemanfaatan lahan makam", described as a permanent right with no expiry ("tanpa ada masa berlaku"). The price includes permanent grass and grave upkeep, and there are no monthly or annual dues. It is issued within 1 month of full payment. **[secondary]**
- **Transfer (pengalihan hak):**
  - allowed as hibah or sale **only while the plot is unused**;
  - requires the original certificate and a Rp 500.000 balik-nama fee;
  - the seller, the buyer and PT Nuansa Usaha Mandiri sign a *Berita Acara Pengalihan Hak atas Pemanfaatan Lahan Makam*.
  - Source: agent pages https://infomakamalazhar.com/pengalihan-hak-lahan-makam/ and makamalazhar.co.id. **[secondary]**
- **Pre-need:** allowed, paid in cash or up to 12-month instalments. **[secondary]**
- **Burial documents:** the deceased's KTP, the death certificate, the plot holder's KTP, and proof of payment. **[secondary]**

**[unverified]** How a "perpetual" right squares with PP 9/1987. That regulation gives non-public cemetery land *Hak Pakai* that must be extended and bars commercial operation. The certificate is a private contractual right against the operator, not a land title, so its durability depends on the operator's own land right and licence.

## 5. Answers to the ticket's questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Jakarta TPU tenure | The IPTM lasts 3 years and can be renewed every 3 years with no stated limit. Renewal is due within 3 months after expiry (Perda DKI 3/2007 Pasal 33). It has been free since Perda 1/2024. |
| Who can extend | The "ahli waris atau penanggung jawab" (Pasal 32–33). This maps to **Pemegang Hak**. |
| Lapse | An unrenewed plot can be layered by strangers without the heirs' consent (jakarta.go.id; Pasal 36(1)). |
| Tumpang | Allowed at least 3 years after the earlier burial, with at least 1 m of soil. Non-family burials need heir consent while the IPTM is active (Pasal 36). Surabaya is similar. Bandung needs no consent at all. |
| Other cities | Surabaya Perda 4/2025, Bandung Perda 5/2023 and Kota Tangerang: the same 3-year renewable model and the same advance-reservation ban. |
| Private parks | Marketed as perpetual under an operator-issued certificate, with no renewal or maintenance fees. Transferable for a fee (SDH Rp 5 juta; Al-Azhar Rp 500 rb, unused plots only). |
| Burial documents | Jakarta: the Lurah's death report, the RS or Puskesmas death letter, and copies of the deceased's KK and KTP; online, also the applicant's KTP and KK. Bandung accepts an RT/RW death letter. |
| Advance purchase in TPU | **No.** Prohibited in Jakarta (Pasal 37), Surabaya (Pasal 24 d) and Bandung (Pasal 12). |
