## 1. What a land wakaf for a cemetery legally requires

### 1.1 The six required elements
UU 41/2004 Pasal 6 lists six elements that every wakaf must have:
- **Wakif**: the person or body giving the land
- **Nazhir**: who receives and manages it
- **Harta benda wakaf**: the asset itself
- **Ikrar wakaf**: the wakif's declaration
- **Peruntukan**: what the asset is for
- **Jangka waktu**: how long the wakaf lasts

A cemetery fits the permitted purposes in Pasal 22 (sarana ibadah / kesejahteraan umum). Land is always given *forever*. PP 42/2006 Pasal 18(1) says: "Benda wakaf tidak bergerak berupa tanah hanya dapat diwakafkan untuk jangka waktu selama-lamanya", with an exception only for HGB or Hak Pakai on someone else's land (Pasal 17(1)d). PMA 73/2013 Pasal 4 says the same.

### 1.2 Wakif and the land
- **Who can be a wakif.** An individual must be adult, of sound mind, legally competent, and the **lawful owner** of the land (UU 41 Pasal 8(1)). Pasal 15: the land must be "dimiliki dan dikuasai oleh Wakif secara sah".
- **Land rights that can be given** (PP 42 Pasal 17(1); Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 3):
  - Hak Milik, registered or not, including tanah milik adat
  - HGB, HGU or Hak Pakai on state land
  - HGB or Hak Pakai on HPL or Hak Milik land, with written consent of the right-holder
  - State land that already has a mosque, musala or **makam** on it (PP 42 Pasal 39(1)e; PMA 73 Pasal 3d)
- **Clean title.** The land must be free of seizure, dispute and mortgage (PP 42 Pasal 17(3); PMA 73 Pasal 5).
- **Consents.** Joint marital property needs the spouse's consent (PMA 73 Pasal 19(3), 24(3)b). Inherited land needs the heirs' consent (Pasal 24(3)c).
- **Partial plots.** If only part of a Hak Milik parcel is given, the certificate has to be measured and split first (PP 42 Pasal 39(1)b; Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 4).
- **Documents at the ikrar.** The wakif hands the land certificate or other proof of ownership to the PPAIW (UU 41 Pasal 19; PP 42 Pasal 28).

### 1.3 Nazhir, registered before the AIW
- **Forms and requirements.** A nazhir can be an individual, an organisation or a legal entity (UU 41 Pasal 9–10).
  - Individual nazhir: Indonesian citizen, **Muslim**, adult, amanah, legally competent.
  - Organisation or badan hukum: its officers must meet the individual requirements, and it must work in social, education, community and/or Islamic religious affairs.
- **Registration is mandatory.** UU 41 Pasal 14(1): "Nazhir harus terdaftar pada Menteri dan Badan Wakaf Indonesia".
  - PP 42 Pasal 4(2), 7(1) and 11(1): registration goes to the Minister and BWI **through the local KUA**.
  - BWI issues the *tanda bukti pendaftaran Nazhir* (PP 42 Pasal 4(4)). The KUA issues the *surat pengesahan Nazhir* (Peraturan BWI 3/2008 Pasal 2(3)–(4)).
  - An organisation nazhir must register **before the AIW is signed** (PP 42 Pasal 7(5)).
- **Individual nazhir.** A group of at least 3 people, and at least one must live in the kecamatan where the land is (PP 42 Pasal 4(5)–(6)).
- **Organisation or badan hukum nazhir** (PP 42 Pasal 7(3), 11(3)). At least one officer must live in the kabupaten/kota where the land is. It must also provide:
  - the notarial deed and AD/ART (for a badan hukum, ratified by the authorities)
  - the list of officers
  - a wakaf work programme
  - a register of wakaf assets kept separate from its own assets
  - a signed statement agreeing to be audited
- **Where BWI registers land nazhir** (BWI knowledge base, 2026), by land area:
  - more than 20,000 m²: BWI Pusat
  - 1,000–20,000 m²: BWI Perwakilan Provinsi
  - less than 1,000 m²: BWI Perwakilan Kabupaten/Kota
- **Duties** (UU 41 Pasal 11; PP 42 Pasal 13): administer, manage and develop, supervise and protect the asset, and report to BWI. PMA 73 Pasal 28 sets reporting **every 6 months** to the Kanwil Kemenag and BWI.
- **Nazhir fee.** At most **10% of net proceeds** (UU 41 Pasal 12). Taking more is a crime under Pasal 67(3). Peraturan BWI 1/2020 Pasal 23 splits net proceeds as: nazhir at most 10%, mauquf alaih at least 50%, the rest to reserves.
- **Replacing a nazhir.** Only BWI can remove or replace a nazhir (UU 41 Pasal 45(2); Peraturan BWI 3/2008). The KUA can propose it if the nazhir has been inactive for a year after the AIW (PP 42 Pasal 6(4), 9(3), 12(3)).
- **The land does not belong to the nazhir.** PP 42 Pasal 3(2): "Terdaftarnya harta benda wakaf atas nama Nazhir tidak membuktikan kepemilikan Nazhir".

### 1.4 Ikrar and the AIW (Akta Ikrar Wakaf) at the KUA
- **Who is the PPAIW.** For land, the PPAIW is the **Kepala KUA** and/or the official in charge of wakaf (PP 42 Pasal 37(1)). A notary appointed as PPAIW by the Minister may also draw up the AIW (Pasal 37(4)–(5); PMA 73 Pasal 27).
- **The ikrar ceremony.** UU 41 Pasal 17(1): the wakif declares the wakaf to the nazhir "di hadapan PPAIW dengan disaksikan oleh 2 (dua) orang saksi". It happens in a **Majelis Ikrar Wakaf** attended by:
  - the nazhir
  - the mauquf alaih
  - at least 2 witnesses

  The mauquf alaih does not have to attend if the beneficiary is the general public (PP 42 Pasal 30(1),(3); Pasal 32).
- **Witnesses.** Adult, Muslim, of sound mind, legally competent (UU 41 Pasal 20).
- **Proxy.** If the wakif cannot attend or speak for a legally valid reason, they may appoint a proxy by power of attorney, confirmed by 2 witnesses (UU 41 Pasal 18).
- **Before the ceremony, the PPAIW checks** the paperwork *and the physical condition of the land* (PP 42 Pasal 34b).
- **Signing and copies.** The AIW is signed by the wakif, the nazhir and the 2 witnesses (and/or the mauquf alaih), then ratified by the PPAIW (PMA 73 Pasal 20(1)). Copies go to 7 parties, including the Kantor Kemenag, the Kantor Pertanahan and BWI (Pasal 20(2)).
- **What the AIW must contain:** identities of the wakif, nazhir and witnesses; details of the asset; peruntukan (mauquf alaih); jangka waktu (PP 42 Pasal 32(4)).
- **Handover of the land.** The land is handed to the nazhir with a *berita acara serah terima* no later than the AIW signing. This is not needed if the AIW already records the handover (PP 42 Pasal 36).
- **The wakaf is final.** UU 41 Pasal 3: "Wakaf yang telah diikrarkan tidak dapat dibatalkan."

### 1.5 Registration at BPN and the wakaf land certificate
- **Filing.** The PPAIW, acting for the nazhir, files the AIW and supporting documents with the Kantor Pertanahan.
- **Deadline: the three regulations disagree.**
  - UU 41 Pasal 32: 7 working days
  - PMA 73 Pasal 20(3): 21 days for the AIW copy
  - Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 2(2): 30 days

  Confirm with the local KUA which one it applies **[unverified which governs in practice]**.
- **Attachments** (PP 42 Pasal 38(2); Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 6(2)):
  - the AIW
  - the Hak Milik certificate and surat ukur
  - the KUA's *surat pengesahan Nazhir*
  - a statement that the land is free of dispute, seizure and mortgage (PP 42 says it must be acknowledged by the kepala desa/lurah and endorsed by the camat)
- **Result.** The original land right "hapus sejak tanggal Ikrar Wakaf" (Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 2(1)). BPN issues a **Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf in the nazhir's name** (Pasal 6(3)). For an organisation or badan hukum, the certificate carries its name and its officers' names (Peraturan BWI 3/2008 Pasal 2(6)).
- **Public register.** The Minister and BWI administer registrations and announce registered wakaf land to the public (UU 41 Pasal 37–38; PP 42 Pasal 44).

### 1.6 Cemetery-specific layer (outside wakaf law)
PP 9/1987 treats a cemetery run by a social or religious body as a **Tempat Pemakaman Bukan Umum (TPBU)**.
- Its location, *"termasuk tanah wakaf"*, is designated by the Bupati/Walikota (Pasal 2(2)).
- Running it needs an izin from the Bupati/Walikota, or the Gubernur in DKI (Pasal 5(3)).
- Wakaf cemetery land keeps Hak Milik status rather than Hak Pakai (Pasal 3(2)).
- Individual graves are at most 2.5 m × 1.5 m (Pasal 4(3)).

So a wakaf cemetery generally needs **local-government permission as well as** the wakaf paperwork. Detailed local rules are in Perda, which ticket 04 covers.

## 2. Can a platform or yayasan that is not the nazhir facilitate it?

**What the law says.** No text grants or forbids a role for a third-party "facilitator" in land wakaf. The legal acts belong to:
- the **wakif**: the ikrar and handing over proof of ownership
- the **nazhir**: accepting the land, registration, management, reporting
- the **PPAIW/KUA**: checking, the AIW, filing with BPN
- **BPN**: the certificate
- **BWI**: nazhir registration and replacement

Anyone else can only help around these acts. Anyone can also report to Kemenag or BWI as "masyarakat" under the supervision provisions (PMA 73 Pasal 31, 33–34).

**Things YIEM (as non-nazhir) can reasonably do.** These are inferences, not stated permissions:
- Explain the process and publish checklists drawn from the texts above.
- Collect an *expression of interest* (the land, the owner, what they intend) and pre-check documents: certificate, KTP, spouse/heir consent, clean-title statement.
- Introduce the wakif to a **registered nazhir** and to the KUA, and track status as the offline steps happen.
- Help prepare documents and scheduling (practical help for the wakif or nazhir).
- Show a status timeline that mirrors the official stages.

**Things YIEM must not do or claim without being the nazhir:**
- **Receive or hold the land.** The land can only be received by the nazhir (UU 41 Pasal 1(4), 17). An intake form must not read like the wakaf is being made "to Makam.co.id / YIEM".
- **Say or imply that submitting the online form *is* the wakaf.** The wakaf only exists legally through the ikrar before the PPAIW and the AIW (Pasal 17, 21). The General Elucidation says acts of wakaf "wajib dicatat dan dituangkan dalam akta ikrar wakaf dan didaftarkan".
- **Issue anything that looks like an AIW, a "sertifikat wakaf" or a surat akad.** Concept step 6 ("penerbitan sertifikat/surat akad") is only done by the PPAIW (the AIW) and BPN (the Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf). The platform can show or store copies. It must not issue them.
- **Promise returns, resale or plot sales on the land**, or decide its use. Peruntukan is set by the wakif in the ikrar (UU 41 Pasal 23), and only BWI can permit changing it (Pasal 44). Changing peruntukan without permission is a crime: up to 4 years or Rp400 million (Pasal 67(2)).
- **Take a share of proceeds as though it were the nazhir.** The 10% cap applies to the nazhir. A facilitator skimming proceeds is legally undefined and risky **[unverified, needs lawyer]**.
- **Collect money "for wakaf"** (for example, to buy cemetery land). That is *wakaf melalui uang* (Peraturan BWI 1/2020 Pasal 1(4)) or wakaf uang. It must go through the nazhir's wakaf account reported to BWI, or an LKS-PWU (UU 41 Pasal 28–30; Peraturan BWI 1/2020 Pasal 6(3)). This is out of scope for v1. Public fundraising permits (PUB) were not researched **[unverified]**.

**If YIEM wants to be the nazhir**, it can: a yayasan is a badan hukum.
- It qualifies if its purpose covers social or Islamic religious work and its officers are Muslim and meet the Pasal 10 requirements.
- It must register through the KUA with BWI, per land (PP 42 Pasal 11; Peraturan BWI 3/2008).
- It must have an officer who lives in the kabupaten/kota of each piece of land.
- It must keep wakaf assets on a separate register, accept audits, report every 6 months, and stay within the 10% fee cap.
- A nazhir may partner with other parties "sesuai dengan prinsip Syariah" (PP 42 Pasal 45(2)), so a "YIEM as partner of a local nazhir" model is also open.

Whether YIEM is currently eligible depends on its deed. Ticket 01/10 should check its AD purpose clause and its officers.

**Suggested honest copy (draft, not legally reviewed):** "Makam.co.id membantu Anda menyiapkan dan memantau proses wakaf tanah. Ikrar wakaf dilakukan di hadapan PPAIW (Kepala KUA) dengan Nazhir terdaftar, dan sertifikat tanah wakaf diterbitkan oleh Kantor Pertanahan."

## 3. Wakaf sosial (khairi) vs wakaf keluarga (ahli)

Both are legally recognised and follow the **same procedure**.
- The UU 41 General Elucidation says the law "tidak memisahkan antara wakaf-ahli ... dengan wakaf-khairi".
- PP 42 Pasal 30(4): the wakif's declaration "dapat dalam bentuk wakaf-khairi atau wakaf-ahli".

| | Wakaf khairi ("sosial") | Wakaf ahli ("keluarga") |
|---|---|---|
| Who benefits (mauquf alaih) | The general public or a group of people, e.g. a public or dhuafa cemetery | "kesejahteraan umum sesama kerabat berdasarkan hubungan darah (nasab) dengan Wakif" (PP 42 Pasal 30(5)), e.g. a family cemetery |
| Mauquf alaih at the Majelis Ikrar | Not required when it is the general public (Pasal 30(3)) | Required to attend (Pasal 30(1)); presumably family representatives **[unverified how KUAs handle this in practice]** |
| Can it be taken back later? | No (UU 41 Pasal 3, 40) | No. It is still a permanent wakaf: it cannot be sold, inherited or given away (Pasal 40). The family gets the *use*, not ownership |
| When the family line ends | n/a | By law it becomes wakaf khairi, and the Minister decides its use on BWI's advice (PP 42 Pasal 30(6)) |
| Nazhir, AIW, certificate | Same | Same. There must still be a registered nazhir (the family can form a 3-person individual nazhir), an AIW and a BPN certificate |

Product implications:
- The concept's "sosial / keluarga" choice maps cleanly to *mauquf alaih*: the public vs the wakif's blood relatives. Label it that way.
- Tell users that a family wakaf cemetery is **not** private family property. It cannot later be sold or divided among heirs.

## 4. Can wakaf cemetery land be managed commercially (wakaf produktif)?

**What wakaf law allows:**
- Nazhir must manage and develop the asset "secara produktif" (UU 41 Pasal 43(2)). The Elucidation lists trade, construction and "usaha-usaha yang tidak bertentangan dengan syariah".
- Nazhir may partner with others (PP 42 Pasal 45(2)).
- Nazhir may ask the wakif for admin or operational fees without reducing the wakaf (Peraturan BWI 1/2020 Pasal 2(3)).

**Hard limits:**
1. **No sale.** The land "dilarang ... dijual ... diwariskan ... dialihkan" (UU 41 Pasal 40), with up to 5 years or Rp500 million under Pasal 67(1). Selling plots as *ownership* of wakaf land is therefore not possible. At most the nazhir can grant *use* of a plot.
2. **Peruntukan binds.** If the AIW says "makam", the nazhir must use the land as a cemetery (UU 41 Pasal 42, 44). Charging for use is productive management only if it is consistent with that purpose. The proceeds go to the mauquf alaih (at least 50%) with the nazhir capped at 10% (Peraturan BWI 1/2020 Pasal 23).
3. **PP 9/1987 Pasal 6(3):** a TPBU (a cemetery run by a social or religious body, which includes wakaf cemeteries) "**tidak dibenarkan dikelola secara komersial**". This is the strongest constraint on "komersial" plot pricing on wakaf land. How local governments and existing operators reconcile it with charging fees is **[unverified; needs ticket 04 / a lawyer]**.

**Market practice (not a legal source):**
- Kemenag's Direktorat Zakat & Wakaf published "Belajar dari Tata Kelola Wakaf Produktif Berbasis Makam Firdaus Memorial Park" (29 Sep 2024): https://kemenag.go.id/nasional/belajar-dari-tata-kelola-wakaf-produktif-berbasis-makam-firdaus-memorial-park-0ujAL. I could read only its metadata, not the body; the page is JS-rendered.
- Its operator describes the model this way: "setiap yang berwakaf akan mendapatkan satu kavling, sekaligus berkontribusi untuk pemakaman dhuafa" (https://www.sinergifoundation.org/wakaf-pemakaman-di-indonesia/).
- So the pattern the government tolerates or promotes appears to be: **a donor pays a *wakaf contribution* (via money), gets the right to be buried in one plot, and the surplus subsidises free burials for the poor.** It is not "buy a plot". How that structure is papered (AIW, wakaf melalui uang, nazhir) was not verified **[unverified]**.

**Implications for concept step 5** ("Penilaian komersial / non-komersial untuk pengelolaan"):
- This decision belongs to the **wakif, in the ikrar** (the peruntukan) and to the **nazhir** (how the land is managed within it). It is not the platform's decision.
- In v1, show step 5 as information the wakif states ("rencana peruntukan") and do not present it as an assessment YIEM makes.
- Any paid plots on wakaf land need legal review against PP 9/1987 Pasal 6(3) and UU 41 Pasal 40 before YIEM lists them for booking.

## 5. The concept's six steps: digital vs in person

| # | Concept step | Who owns it legally | Can be digital? | Must be in person / offline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pengajuan**: choose sosial or keluarga | Wakif (intent); final in the ikrar | **Yes.** Intake form choosing khairi (public) or ahli (blood relatives), with a note that the choice becomes binding only in the AIW | none |
| 2 | **Pengisian data**: owner, location, ownership documents | Wakif | **Yes.** Upload certificate, KTP, spouse/heir consent, location pin and photos. Kemenag's own **SIWAK / e-AIW** already takes online registration and uploads (KTP, bukti kepemilikan, foto lokasi) that the KUA then verifies (Kemenag Bengkulu, 19 Feb 2026). The platform can pre-check and later point the user to SIWAK | Originals are shown to the PPAIW (UU 41 Pasal 19) |
| 3 | **Verifikasi & survei**: field check and legality | PPAIW checks paperwork and physical condition (PP 42 Pasal 34b); BPN measures (Permen ATR 2/2017 Pasal 4, 6) | Only status tracking, plus an optional informal pre-check by YIEM staff | **Field visit and measurement.** The clean-title statement needs kepala desa/lurah and camat sign-off (PP 42 Pasal 38(2)b) |
| 4 | **Administrasi**: permits and akad wakaf | Nazhir registration (KUA→BWI); **ikrar in the Majelis Ikrar Wakaf before the PPAIW with 2 witnesses** (UU 41 Pasal 17; PP 42 Pasal 30, 32); TPBU permit from Bupati/Walikota (PP 9/1987 Pasal 5(3)) | Status tracking. Nazhir-registration paperwork may partly go through SIWAK **[unverified]** | **Ikrar and AIW signing are in person**; the wakif may use a proxy (UU 41 Pasal 18). I found no rule allowing a remote or video ikrar **[unverified; e-AIW digitises the deed and filing, not attendance as far as found]** |
| 5 | **Penilaian**: commercial or non-commercial use | Peruntukan is set by the wakif in the ikrar (UU 41 Pasal 23); management by the nazhir under BWI rules | Record the stated plan only | Not a platform decision (see §4) |
| 6 | **Serah terima**: documentation, certificate/akad, management | Handover *berita acara* at the AIW (PP 42 Pasal 36); PPAIW files with BPN; BPN issues the Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf in the nazhir's name | Track status, store copies of the AIW and certificate, and notify the applicant | Handover happens at the Majelis. The **certificate is issued by BPN, not by the platform** |

This fits v1's decision that the platform does intake and status tracking while survey, akad and certificate stay offline.

A status model that mirrors the legal stages:
1. Diajukan
2. Dokumen diperiksa
3. Nazhir ditetapkan/terdaftar
4. Dijadwalkan ikrar di KUA
5. AIW terbit
6. Didaftarkan ke BPN
7. Sertifikat Tanah Wakaf terbit

Plus Ditolak/Ditunda with a reason.
