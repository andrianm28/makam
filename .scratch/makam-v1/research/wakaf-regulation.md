# Wakaf tanah regulation and the platform's role

Research for [issue 03](../issues/03-wakaf-regulation.md). Researched 2026-09-24 against primary legal texts (full text read unless flagged otherwise).

## TL;DR

- A wakaf of land needs a **Wakif** who lawfully owns land that is free of disputes and liens. The Wakif makes an **ikrar** to a **Nazhir** before a **PPAIW** (the Kepala KUA, or a Notaris appointed as PPAIW) with 2 witnesses. The PPAIW records it in an **Akta Ikrar Wakaf (AIW)**, then registers the land at the **Kantor Pertanahan (BPN)**, which issues a **Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf in the Nazhir's name**. The Nazhir must be registered with the Kemenag and BWI through the KUA. A wakaf of land is permanent and cannot be revoked, sold, inherited, pledged or swapped without permission.
- **Only a registered Nazhir can receive and manage the land.** No regulation creates a "facilitator" role, and none forbids one either. A platform that is not the Nazhir can help with intake, documents and scheduling. It cannot receive the land, sign the AIW, issue certificates, or take in cash "wakaf" in its own name.
- **Wakaf keluarga = wakaf ahli** (PP 42/2006 Art. 30). It benefits the Wakif's blood relatives, its beneficiaries attend the majelis ikrar, and it becomes public (khairi) wakaf once the family line dies out. **Wakaf sosial = wakaf khairi.**
- **The law requires productive management of wakaf land**, and the Nazhir's cut is capped at 10% of net proceeds. But a cemetery run by a social or religious body (a *Tempat Pemakaman Bukan Umum*) **"tidak dibenarkan dikelola secara komersial"** (may not be run commercially) under PP 9/1987 Art. 6(3), which is still in force. Selling burial plots on wakaf land in any sense that transfers the land is a crime (UU 41 Arts. 40 and 67).
- **Digital vs in person.** Intake, document upload, status tracking and even the KUA's own **e-AIW/SIWAK** filing can be digital. The **majelis ikrar in front of the PPAIW with witnesses, the field survey, and BPN registration and certification** stay offline and in the hands of the officials.

## Part A: Facts (cited)

Source keys (e.g. `[UU41]`) are defined in the [Sources](#sources) section.

### A1. What a wakaf of land legally requires

**Elements.** A wakaf needs a Wakif, a Nazhir, the property, an ikrar, a stated purpose (peruntukan) and a duration [UU41 Art. 6]. It is valid if it follows syariah [UU41 Art. 2], and **once pledged it cannot be revoked** [UU41 Art. 3].

**Wakif.** A Wakif can be a person, an organisation or a legal entity. A person must be an adult, of sound mind, legally capable, and **the lawful owner** of the property [UU41 Arts. 7-8]. The property must be "dimiliki dan dikuasai oleh Wakif secara sah" (lawfully owned and held by the Wakif) [UU41 Art. 15]. If the land is marital property, the Wakif needs the spouse's consent. If it is inherited property, the heirs must consent [PMA73 Art. 19(3), Art. 24(3)].

**Which land qualifies:**
- Hak milik, registered or not
- HGB, HGU or hak pakai on state land
- HGB or hak pakai on HPL or hak milik land, with written consent from the rights-holder
- Hak milik atas satuan rumah susun

The land must be "bebas dari segala sitaan, perkara, sengketa, dan tidak dijaminkan" (free of seizure, litigation and disputes, and not pledged as collateral) [PP42 Art. 17]. **Land can only be pledged in perpetuity.** The exception is HGB or hak pakai on HPL or hak milik land [PP42 Art. 18(1)], which Kemenag treats as lasting only as long as the underlying right [PMA73 Art. 4]. Permen ATR 2/2017 also lets **state land** be pledged. PP 42 Art. 39(1)(e) and PMA 73 Art. 3(d) specifically allow "tanah negara yang di atasnya berdiri bangunan masjid, musala, **makam**" (state land on which a mosque, prayer room or **grave** stands) to be registered as wakaf [PP42 Art. 39(1)(e); PMA73 Art. 3(d); ATR2 Art. 3(1)(e), Art. 11].

**Ikrar and AIW:**
- The ikrar is made "oleh Wakif kepada Nazhir **di hadapan PPAIW** dengan disaksikan oleh 2 (dua) orang saksi" (by the Wakif to the Nazhir, before the PPAIW, witnessed by two people) and is recorded in an AIW [UU41 Art. 17].
- If the Wakif cannot attend "karena alasan yang dibenarkan oleh hukum" (for a legally accepted reason), they can appoint a proxy by power of attorney, confirmed by 2 witnesses [UU41 Art. 18].
- The Wakif hands the proof of ownership to the PPAIW [UU41 Art. 19].
- Witnesses must be adult, Muslim, of sound mind and legally capable [UU41 Art. 20].
- The ikrar takes place in a **Majelis Ikrar Wakaf attended by the Nazhir, the Mauquf alaih (beneficiary) and at least 2 witnesses** [PP42 Art. 30(1)]. Mauquf alaih attendance is not required when the beneficiary is the general public [PP42 Art. 30(3)].
- The PPAIW checks the administrative requirements **and the physical condition of the property**. The ikrar and AIW are valid only if made in the Majelis [PP42 Art. 34(b)-(c)]. The Wakif, the Nazhir, 2 witnesses and/or the Mauquf alaih sign the AIW, and the PPAIW ratifies it [PP42 Art. 34(d); PMA73 Art. 20(1)].
- The property is handed over with a berita acara serah terima (handover record) **no later than the signing of the AIW**. No separate record is needed if the AIW already states the handover [PP42 Art. 36].
- The minimum contents of an AIW are listed in [UU41 Art. 21] and [PP42 Art. 32(4)]. The form is set by the Dirjen [PMA73 Art. 23].

**Who is the PPAIW for land.** The PPAIW is the **Kepala KUA and/or the official handling wakaf affairs**. This "does not prevent a Wakif from making the AIW before a Notaris" [PP42 Art. 37(1), (4)]. A Notaris must be appointed as PPAIW by Keputusan Menteri [PMA73 Art. 27].

**Registration and certificate:**
- "PPAIW atas nama Nazhir mendaftarkan harta benda wakaf kepada Instansi yang berwenang" (the PPAIW registers the property with the competent authority on the Nazhir's behalf) [UU41 Art. 32]. For land, that authority is **BPN** [UU41 Elucidation of Art. 32].
- Once the land is pledged, the original land right ends and the land becomes wakaf property [ATR2 Art. 2(1)].
- The Kantor Pertanahan issues a **Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf atas nama Nazhir** (land-wakaf certificate in the Nazhir's name) [ATR2 Arts. 6-11; PP42 Art. 39].
- For registered hak milik land, the application needs:
  - a letter of application
  - the surat ukur (survey map)
  - the SHM (hak milik certificate)
  - the AIW or APAIW
  - the **Nazhir's surat pengesahan (validation letter) from the KUA**
  - the Nazhir's statement that the land is not in dispute [ATR2 Art. 6(2)]
- If only part of a hak milik plot is pledged, it must first be measured and the certificate split [ATR2 Art. 4; PP42 Art. 39(1)(b)].
- Unregistered customary (adat) land goes directly to a keputusan penegasan (confirmation decision) and then a certificate [ATR2 Art. 7].
- Kemenag and BWI keep the wakaf register and announce registered wakaf property publicly [UU41 Arts. 37-38; PP42 Art. 44].
- The Kantor Pertanahan is covered by electronic certificates. Permen ATR/BPN 3/2023 on electronic documents in land registration lists *tanah wakaf* among the registrable objects [ATR3-2023]. **(Unverified: I did not read the full text of Permen ATR/BPN 3/2023. The claim rests on its title page and a search summary of Art. 15.)**

**Deadlines. These conflict across the regulations; flagged.**

| Regulation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| UU 41 Art. 32 | 7 hari kerja (working days) after the AIW is signed, for the PPAIW to register |
| PMA 73 Art. 20(3) | 21 days to deliver the AIW copy to the Kantor Pertanahan |
| Permen ATR 2/2017 Art. 2(2) | 30 days for the PPAIW to submit the AIW to the Kantor Pertanahan |

The deadline is the PPAIW's obligation, not the Nazhir's or a facilitator's.

**Protection once pledged:**
- Wakaf property may not be pledged as collateral, seized, given away, **sold**, inherited, swapped or otherwise transferred [UU41 Art. 40].
- Swaps are allowed only for public-interest use under the spatial plan, with the Minister's written permission and BWI's consent [UU41 Art. 41]. The procedure was rewritten in 2018 [PP25 Arts. 49-51A]. For wakaf up to 5,000 m², permission is delegated to the Kanwil Kemenag [PP25 Art. 49(3)].
- Changing the peruntukan (designated purpose) requires BWI's written permission [UU41 Art. 44].
- Penalties: up to 5 years and/or Rp500 million for selling or transferring wakaf property; 4 years / Rp400 million for an unpermitted change of purpose; 3 years / Rp300 million for taking more than the 10% Nazhir cut [UU41 Art. 67].

### A2. Nazhir: who can be one and how registration works

**Who can be a Nazhir:**
- A group of individuals: at least 3 people, one of whom lives in the kecamatan (subdistrict) where the land is [PP42 Art. 4(5)-(6)].
- An organisation, or a **legal entity (badan hukum) working in "sosial, pendidikan, kemasyarakatan, dan/atau keagamaan Islam"** (social, educational, community and/or Islamic religious work) whose board members meet the individual criteria: Indonesian citizen, Muslim, adult, trustworthy, physically and mentally able, legally capable [UU41 Arts. 9-10].
- A legal-entity Nazhir also needs one board member domiciled in the kabupaten/kota where the land is. It must have:
  - its notarial deed of establishment with legal approval
  - its board list
  - its ART (bylaws)
  - a **wakaf work programme**
  - a **separate list of wakaf assets**
  - a statement agreeing to be audited [PP42 Art. 11(3)]

**Registration:**
- Nazhirs "wajib didaftarkan pada Menteri dan BWI melalui Kantor Urusan Agama setempat" (must be registered with the Minister and BWI through the local KUA) [PP42 Arts. 4(2), 7(1), 11(1); UU41 Art. 14(1)].
- **For organisations, registration must happen before the AIW is signed** [PP42 Art. 7(5)]. PP 42 states this for organisations only. For legal entities, ATR 2/2017 still requires the KUA's surat pengesahan before certification.
- **BWI issues the tanda bukti pendaftaran Nazhir** (proof of registration), and **the KUA issues the surat pengesahan Nazhir** (validation letter) [PerBWI3-2008 Art. 2(3)-(4)].
- For an organisation or legal-entity Nazhir, the certificate is issued in the name of the organisation "dan nama pengurusnya" (and its board members' names) [PerBWI3-2008 Art. 2(6)].
- BWI's public guidance says land-wakaf Nazhir registration is tiered by area: **>20,000 m² at BWI Pusat, 1,000-20,000 m² at BWI Provinsi, <1,000 m² at BWI Kabupaten/Kota** [BWI-Nazhir]. **(Guidance page, not a regulation. I found no regulation stating these tiers.)**

**Duties:**
- Administer, manage, develop, supervise and protect the wakaf property, and report to BWI [UU41 Art. 11; PP42 Art. 13].
- Report to the Kanwil Kemenag and BWI every 6 months [PMA73 Art. 28].
- Report annually to BWI on non-cash wakaf by 31 March. Failing to report can lead to suspension or revocation of the proof of registration [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 24].
- If the Nazhir has not carried out its duties within 1 year of the AIW, the Kepala KUA can propose its replacement to BWI [PP42 Arts. 9(3), 12(3)].
- The fixed 5-year Nazhir term (PP42 Art. 14) **was repealed** by PP 25/2018 [PP25 item 2].

### A3. Can a non-Nazhir platform or yayasan facilitate?

- The regulations name only these actors in a land wakaf: Wakif (or the Wakif's proxy), Nazhir, Mauquf alaih, witnesses, PPAIW (KUA or Notaris), Kantor Pertanahan, Kemenag, BWI, and the kepala desa/lurah/camat for the no-dispute statement [UU41; PP42 Arts. 30-39; ATR2 Art. 6]. **None of them is an intermediary or facilitator. Nothing I read prohibits one either.**
- The Nazhir "dapat bekerjasama dengan pihak lain sesuai dengan prinsip syariah" (may cooperate with other parties in line with syariah) in managing and developing wakaf property [PP42 Art. 45(2)]. BWI and the Minister may cooperate with "pihak lain yang dipandang perlu" (other parties as they see fit) [UU41 Arts. 49(2), 64].
- **Money is different:** when PP 42 took effect, "lembaga non keuangan atau perseorangan yang menerima wakaf uang wajib untuk mengalihkan penerimaan wakaf uang melalui rekening wadi'ah pada LKS-PWU" (non-financial bodies or individuals receiving cash wakaf must route it through a wadi'ah account at an LKS-PWU) [PP42 Art. 58(2)(a)]. Cash wakaf may only be received through a cash-wakaf account in the Nazhir's name, reported to BWI [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 6(3)].
  - Money given to **buy land** for a wakaf is "Wakaf Melalui Uang". The Nazhir receives it directly or through an LKS-PWU account in the Nazhir's name, and **the Nazhir** issues the receipt, with copies to BWI and Kemenag [PerBWI1-2020 Arts. 1(4), 20-21].
  - Kemenag replaced the cash-wakaf procedure with **PMA 14/2025** (in force 9 Oct 2025). Reports say it tightens licensing for Nazhirs and LKSs and makes digital AIW/SWU valid [PMA14-2025]. **(Unverified: I did not read its text. It concerns cash wakaf, not land.)**
- The Nazhir's income is capped: "imbalan dari hasil bersih ... tidak melebihi 10%" (a fee from net proceeds of no more than 10%) [UU41 Art. 12]. BWI splits net proceeds as Nazhir ≤10%, Mauquf alaih ≥50%, remainder to reserves [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 23(2)]. The Nazhir "dapat meminta biaya administrasi dan/atau biaya operasional kepada Wakif dengan tidak mengurangi wakaf" (may ask the Wakif for administrative or operating costs, as long as the wakaf itself is not reduced) [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 2(3)].

### A4. Wakaf sosial vs wakaf keluarga (khairi vs ahli)

- "Pernyataan kehendak Wakif ... dapat dalam bentuk wakaf-khairi atau wakaf-ahli" (the Wakif's declaration can take the form of wakaf khairi or wakaf ahli) [PP42 Art. 30(4)].
- **Wakaf ahli** "diperuntukkan bagi kesejahteraan umum sesama kerabat berdasarkan hubungan darah (nasab) dengan Wakif" (is for the welfare of the Wakif's blood relatives) [PP42 Art. 30(5)].
- When those relatives have died out, the wakaf ahli **"karena hukum beralih statusnya menjadi wakaf khairi"** (by law becomes wakaf khairi), with its purpose set by the Minister on BWI's advice [PP42 Art. 30(6)].
- Procedural difference: the Mauquf alaih must attend the majelis ikrar **unless the beneficiary is the public** [PP42 Art. 30(1), (3)]. A wakaf ahli names specific beneficiaries, so they (or their representatives) attend. A wakaf khairi for a public cemetery does not need them.
- Everything else is the same for both types: the land is perpetual [PP42 Art. 18(1)], it is registered in the **Nazhir's** name and not the family's [PP42 Art. 3(1)-(2)], and the ban on sale and inheritance applies [UU41 Art. 40]. The regulations do not bar a family member from being among the Nazhir (individual Nazhir criteria, UU41 Art. 10).
- The allowed purposes include "sarana dan kegiatan ibadah" (places and activities of worship) and "kemajuan kesejahteraan umum lainnya" (other public welfare) [UU41 Art. 22]. **The regulations do not list cemeteries as a purpose**, but cemeteries on state land are recognised as registrable wakaf (A1).

### A5. Can wakaf land be managed commercially (wakaf produktif) for burial plots?

- **Wakaf law pushes towards productive use:**
  - Management "dilakukan secara produktif" (is carried out productively) [UU41 Art. 43(2)]. The Elucidation's examples include investment, partnership, trade, building shops and offices, and "usaha-usaha yang tidak bertentangan dengan syariah" (any business not contrary to syariah) [UU41 Elucidation of Art. 43(2)].
  - Management must follow BWI regulations [PP42 Art. 48(1)], i.e. PerBWI 1/2020 [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 2(2)].
  - Management must stay within the peruntukan stated in the AIW [UU41 Art. 42; PP42 Art. 45(1)].
- **Cemetery law restricts it:**
  - Non-public cemeteries (*Tempat Pemakaman Bukan Umum*) are run by social or religious bodies, with a permit from the Bupati/Walikota, or the Governor in Jakarta [PP9 Art. 5(3)].
  - For such cemeteries, **"tidak dibenarkan dikelola secara komersial"** (they may not be run commercially) [PP9 Art. 6(3)].
  - Designating land, **"termasuk tanah wakaf"** (including wakaf land), as a non-public cemetery is done by the Kepala Daerah with the Minister of Home Affairs' approval [PP9 Art. 2(2)].
  - Wakaf cemetery land keeps **Hak Milik** status. Other non-public cemetery land gets Hak Pakai [PP9 Art. 3(2)].
  - A grave may be at most 2.5 m × 1.5 m [PP9 Art. 4(3)].
  - BPK lists PP 9/1987 as **Berlaku** (in force), with no amendment or revocation [PP9-BPK].
- **What the regulations do not settle:** no text I read says whether a Nazhir may charge families for a *right of use* of a plot, maintenance or burial services on wakaf land. They also do not say where "operating cost recovery" ends and "commercial management" begins. Some private operators market "wakaf" plots for a fixed contribution that includes an operating component (e.g. an advertised Rp30 million including 20% operations) [Market-Firdaus]. **(Marketing claim, not a legal source. The operators' legal structure is unverified.)** Issue 04 covers plot-tenure rules generally.

### A6. What is digital today

- **UU ITE:** its rules on electronic information and documents "tidak berlaku untuk ... surat beserta dokumennya yang menurut Undang-Undang harus dibuat dalam bentuk akta notaril atau akta yang dibuat oleh pejabat pembuat akta" (do not apply to documents that the law requires to be a notarial deed or a deed made by a deed official) [UU-ITE Art. 5(4)(b)]. **(Checked against the 2008 original. I did not check whether UU 19/2016 or UU 1/2024 changed Art. 5(4).)**
- **Kemenag runs SIWAK and e-AIW:**
  - Kemenag reported 7,551 electronic AIWs issued since 2022 [Kemenag-eAIW] (headline and date only; the article body did not load).
  - Kanwil Bengkulu (Feb 2026) describes wakif/nazhir **registering online in SIWAK, uploading documents and filling in the e-AIW**, which **KUA staff then verify and validate** [Kemenag-Bengkulu].
  - Kanwil Jabar (15 Sep 2025) reports an ikrar held **in person** with the Wakif, Nazhir, two witnesses and the Kepala KUA as PPAIW. The documentation was then uploaded to SIWAK [Kemenag-Jabar].
  - **(These are government news items, not regulations. I found no regulation that allows a remote ikrar for land.)**
- **BPN** issues electronic certificates under Permen ATR/BPN 3/2023 (see A1 caveat).
- **Cash wakaf** can already be paid through e-banking channels [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 7(3), Art. 9], but only for perpetual, public-benefit wakaf [PerBWI1-2020 Art. 9(2)].

## Part C: Interpretation and recommendations (not legal advice)

These are my readings of the facts above. A wakaf lawyer or the local KUA/BWI office should confirm them before launch.

1. **The platform's legal role is facilitator and record-keeper, not a party to the wakaf.** Unless YIEM registers as a Nazhir, it should never appear on the AIW or certificate, never "receive" land, and never describe itself as the one who "menerima wakaf" (receives the wakaf).
   - YIEM could become a Nazhir badan hukum if its AD covers "sosial ... dan/atau keagamaan Islam" and it meets PP 42 Art. 11(3). That includes a board member in each kabupaten where the land is, a separate wakaf-asset register, and a willingness to be audited.
   - That is a real operational commitment: perpetual management, 6-monthly and annual reporting, and possible replacement by BWI. Issue 10 should decide it with the facts from issue 01.
2. **The v1 scope already set in the map fits the law.** Intake plus status tracking is digital. Survey, ikrar and certificate stay offline. The status machine could mirror the legal milestones:
   `diajukan → dokumen diperiksa → nazhir ditetapkan/terdaftar → jadwal ikrar di KUA → AIW terbit → diajukan ke BPN → sertipikat wakaf terbit`.
   Staff enter the status manually from paper or SIWAK evidence. v1 can't integrate with SIWAK or BPN.
3. **Don't collect money labelled "wakaf" through the platform** in v1. Cash for buying land is Wakaf Melalui Uang, and it must go to an account in the **Nazhir's** name, or an LKS-PWU account for cash wakaf. PMA 14/2025 appears to tighten this further. Fees for the platform's own facilitation service (if any) should be clearly separate from the wakaf and never deducted from it.
4. **Copy and marketing must avoid three claims:**
   - that the platform issues the AIW or certificate
   - that wakaf land plots are "sold" or "owned"
   - that the land will be run for profit

   PP 9/1987 Art. 6(3) and UU 41 Arts. 40 and 67 make those risky. Safer wording: "kami membantu menyiapkan dokumen dan menjadwalkan ikrar wakaf di KUA; akta ikrar dibuat oleh PPAIW dan sertipikat wakaf diterbitkan BPN atas nama nazhir" (we help prepare the documents and schedule the ikrar at the KUA; the PPAIW makes the AIW and BPN issues the wakaf certificate in the Nazhir's name).
5. **Wakaf keluarga is a legal form, not a private family cemetery.** Copy should make clear that the land still belongs to the wakaf, is registered to a Nazhir, can't be inherited or sold, and becomes public once the family line ends. Offer it as a form choice at step 1 and record who the Mauquf alaih are, because they attend the ikrar.
6. **Reorder the concept flow:** put "Penilaian/peruntukan" before "Administrasi/akad", since the peruntukan is fixed in the AIW.
7. **Pricing Petak Makam on wakaf land** should be decided after legal advice. The defensible reading combines PerBWI 1/2020 Art. 2(3) (operating costs from the Wakif) with PP 9/1987 (no commercial non-public cemetery). Under that reading, charges on wakaf cemeteries cover cost-based services such as burial and maintenance, not a price for the plot. Feed this into issues 04 and 05.
