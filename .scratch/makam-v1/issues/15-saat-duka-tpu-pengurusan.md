# Pemesanan Saat Duka at a DKI TPU via Pengurusan

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Map: ../map.md

## Question

When a family asks makam.co.id to arrange a burial at a DKI TPU (Pengurusan), how does the flow in [concept.md](../concept.md) (Cari Lokasi → Pilih Makam → Isi Data → Konfirmasi → Pembayaran → Bukti) adapt, given that the TPU assigns the plot and the permit is filed on JakEVO (1 working day, government fee Rp 0)?

- What does the Pemesan choose instead of a Jenis Makam (a TPU plus a placement wish, e.g. tumpang on a family grave)?
- Who confirms, and within what time: Admin YIEM filing online, or Petugas YIEM going in person?
- Which documents must come before filing, given that the IPTM checklist needs the Puskesmas/RS death certificate, the Kelurahan death report letter and a surat pengantar from the TPU ([research §2.6](../research/cemetery-plot-regulation.md))?
- What happens outside working hours or at weekends, when JakEVO or PTSP does not process?
- Does YIEM's Pengurusan fee follow the same pay-after-the-burial rule as a Lokasi Mitra ([Speed and documents for Pemesanan Saat Duka](./08-saat-duka-urgency.md))?

## Answer

Decided with the user on 2026-09-25 (all recommendations accepted). Facts from [dki-tpu-burial-sequence.md](../research/dki-tpu-burial-sequence.md) and [cemetery-plot-regulation.md §2](../research/cemetery-plot-regulation.md). Items marked _(default)_ were filled in to complete the concept flow and follow earlier decisions.

**Ground truth the flow is built on**: at a DKI TPU the family goes to the TPU, the TPU assigns the plot and the burial happens the same day (TPUs bury daily incl. holidays, 06:00–18:00); the TPU's surat pengantar and the IPTM (JakEVO, 1 working day, Rp 0, proxy allowed with surat kuasa) come **after** the burial. Perda 3/2007 Pasal 16 says the reverse; v1 follows practice (legal compliance is out of scope). The IPTM is the only document YIEM produces.

1. **Cari Lokasi / Pilih Makam**: the Pemesan chooses a **TPU** and a burial type:
   - **Baru** (new plot, assigned by the TPU): only TPUs flagged "menerima makam baru" are offered. Admin YIEM keeps that flag per TPU by hand from Distamhut's ketersediaan perpetakan list (in Oct 2025, 69 of 80 TPUs took tumpang only).
   - **Tumpang** on an existing grave: the Pemesan describes it (TPU, block/number, the Almarhum already buried there, the Pemegang Hak named on its IPTM) and uploads a photo of the IPTM. The form warns that the IPTM must be active, that the previous burial must be ≥3 years old, and that a non-family grave needs the Pemegang Hak's written consent (Pasal 36).
   - A block class (AA.I / A.I …) or placement is an optional free-text wish only.
2. **Eligibility**: Isi Data asks "KTP DKI?" and "Meninggal di Jakarta?". No/no is blocked with a pointer to Lokasi Mitra (Pasal 3). DKI resident + died outside Jakarta adds the Pasal 17(2) documents to the checklist.
3. **Isi Data**: the Pemesan's name + WhatsApp number, the Almarhum's name + date of death, the two eligibility answers, and the **Pemegang Hak** (the ahli waris / penanggung jawab who will be named on the IPTM; defaults to the Pemesan, editable). That person signs the surat kuasa and supplies the KTP/KK. TPU Layanan flagged "bisa hari-H" (fulfilled by Mitra Jasa) can be added, as in "Layanan catalog and Paket Layanan model".
4. **Who does what**:
   1. **Admin YIEM** contacts the TPU staff (WhatsApp/phone) to secure a plot (or confirm the tumpang) and a burial time. If the TPU refuses or is full, Admin YIEM offers another TPU before confirming.
   2. Admin YIEM **confirms** to the Pemesan.
   3. The family buries on the day; YIEM does not attend in v1.
   4. **Petugas YIEM** collects the TPU's original surat pengantar after the burial.
   5. **Admin YIEM** files the IPTM on JakEVO as proxy.
   6. The IPTM scan and expiry are stored on a new **TPU plot record** (TPU + block/number + Almarhum + Pemegang Hak), the same record Perpanjangan uses ("How Perpanjangan verifies the Pemegang Hak"). For a tumpang, the IPTM follows the existing IPTM's expiry and updates that record.
5. **Konfirmasi time**: Admin YIEM confirms within **2 hours, 06:00–18:00, every day** (YIEM needs a daily rota incl. weekends). Submissions are accepted 24/7; the submission screen shows YIEM's on-call contact; night submissions are confirmed by 07:00. IPTM filing waits for the next working day (PTSP Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00) and never holds up the burial.
6. **Konfirmasi content** _(default, per concept.md)_: Nomor Pemesanan; TPU name and address; burial type and agreed time; contacts of Admin YIEM and of the TPU staff; both document lists (see 7); price lines; status.
7. **Documents**, in two moments:
   - **For the burial** (brought by the family to the TPU): the RS/Puskesmas death certificate and the Almarhum's KTP + KK; for tumpang also the old IPTM.
   - **For the IPTM filing** (uploaded to the platform): the Kelurahan death report letter, the surat kuasa generated by the platform (bermaterai, signed by the Pemegang Hak), the Pemegang Hak's KTP + KK, and the Pasal 17(2) letters when the death was outside Jakarta.
   - Uploads are due within **7 days** of the burial. YIEM files within 1 working day of a complete set. After 7 days, Admin YIEM follows up by hand (no written deadline exists for a first IPTM).
8. **Status** _(default)_: Diajukan → Dikonfirmasi → Dimakamkan (Admin YIEM marks it after checking with the TPU/family) → Dokumen Lengkap → IPTM Diajukan → IPTM Terbit. Also Dibatalkan.
9. **Pembayaran**: the same rule as Lokasi Mitra. The invoice is issued at confirmation and due **3×24 h after the burial**. It shows the Pemda retribusi as its own line (Rp 0 today) and **YIEM's Pengurusan fee** labelled as a service fee, not a government charge. There is no Biaya Layanan Platform on TPU orders ("Money flow and revenue model"). The IPTM is filed and its scan handed over **regardless of payment**, since withholding a government permit would look like the paid middlemen Distamhut warns against. **YIEM carries the risk** of non-payment. The Pengurusan fee is one fixed amount set by Admin YIEM _(default, per "YIEM's current assets and records")_.
10. **Cancellation** _(default)_: before the IPTM is filed, the Pemesan may cancel and the invoice is voided (added Layanan follow the cancellation rule for Layanan). Once filed, the full fee is due.
11. **Bukti Pemesanan** is issued once payment settles. The **IPTM scan** is the official permit and is sent to the Pemesan when issued.
12. **Free DIY option**: the TPU page states plainly that families can do this themselves for free at the TPU and PTSP, with a short guide. YIEM's fee is presented as a convenience service.

**Checked against the ticket's questions**: what is chosen → 1; who confirms and in what time → 4–5; documents before filing → 7; outside hours / weekends → 5; the pay-after-burial rule → 9.
