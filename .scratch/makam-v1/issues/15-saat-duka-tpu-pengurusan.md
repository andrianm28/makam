Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

When a family asks makam.co.id to arrange a burial at a DKI TPU (Pengurusan), how does the flow in [concept.md](../concept.md) (Cari Lokasi → Pilih Makam → Isi Data → Konfirmasi → Pembayaran → Bukti) adapt, given that the TPU assigns the plot and the permit is filed on JakEVO (1 working day, government fee Rp 0)?

- What does the Pemesan choose instead of a Jenis Makam (a TPU plus a placement wish, e.g. tumpang on a family grave)?
- Who confirms, and within what time: Admin YIEM filing online, or Petugas YIEM going in person?
- Which documents must come before filing, given that the IPTM checklist needs the Puskesmas/RS death certificate, the Kelurahan death report letter and a surat pengantar from the TPU ([research §2.6](../research/cemetery-plot-regulation.md))?
- What happens outside working hours or at weekends, when JakEVO or PTSP does not process?
- Does YIEM's Pengurusan fee follow the same pay-after-the-burial rule as a Lokasi Mitra ([Speed and documents for Pemesanan Saat Duka](./08-saat-duka-urgency.md))?
