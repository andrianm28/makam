# Regulation of cemetery plots and tenure

Type: research
Status: resolved
Map: ../map.md

## Question

What rules govern the right to use a plot in Indonesia: at least the Jakarta regional regulation on public cemeteries (TPU) and its 'izin penggunaan tanah makam' (3-year tenure, extensions, tumpang/layered burials), how other large cities handle it, and how private cemeteries (e.g. San Diego Hills, Al-Azhar Memorial Garden) structure tenure (perpetual vs fixed term, transfer of rights). What documents are required for a burial (surat keterangan kematian, KTP, KK, letter from the RT/RW)? Is buying a plot in advance allowed in a TPU?

## Answer

- PP 9/1987 covers only land status, plot size (max 2.5 × 1.5 m) and who may run a cemetery. Tenure is set by each region's Perda.
- Jakarta (Perda 3/2007): an IPTM lasts 3 years and can be renewed every 3 years, with the application due within 3 months of expiry. It is free since Perda 1/2024 and applied for online via JakEVO. A plot without an active IPTM can be tumpang'd by strangers without the family's consent. Tumpang otherwise needs ≥3 years since the last burial, and written consent if the new body is not family.
- Buying a TPU plot in advance is banned in Jakarta, Surabaya (Perda 4/2025) and Bandung (Perda 5/2023). Private cemeteries (San Diego Hills, Al Azhar) sell perpetual, one-payment usage rights pre-need that can be transferred before use (agent-sourced, unverified).
- Burial documents: a death certificate from the RS/Puskesmas, a death letter from the Lurah (Jakarta) or RT/RW (Bandung), and the KTP + KK. Perpanjangan in Jakarta needs the applicant's KTP/KK, the old IPTM and a letter from the TPU. Yayasan offering cemetery services in DKI need an izin operasional (Pasal 10).

Second, independent report: [research/04-cemetery-plot-regulation.md](../research/04-cemetery-plot-regulation.md). Adds: Jakarta IPTM free since Perda DKI 1/2024, filed online (Jakevo) in 1 day; only ahli waris/penanggung jawab may apply (matches Pemegang Hak); lapsed plots can be tumpang'd without heirs' consent; advance purchase in a TPU is also prohibited in Surabaya (Perda 4/2025) and Bandung (Perda 5/2023), so Pemesanan Terencana only works at private/wakaf Lokasi; San Diego Hills and Al-Azhar sell perpetual rights via an operator-issued certificate (not BPN), with transfer fees of Rp 5 jt and Rp 500 rb respectively.
