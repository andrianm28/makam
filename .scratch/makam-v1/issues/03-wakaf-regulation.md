## Question

Under UU 41/2004, PP 42/2006 and BWI regulations: what does wakaf of land for a cemetery legally require (AIW/PPAIW at KUA, land certificate, nazhir registration)? Can a platform or yayasan that is **not** the nazhir facilitate it, and what can it say or do? What differs between 'wakaf sosial' and 'wakaf keluarga' (wakaf ahli)? Can wakaf land be managed commercially (wakaf produktif) for burial plots? Which steps in the concept doc's six-step flow can be digital and which must be in person?

## Answer

- Land wakaf needs: Wakif who lawfully owns dispute-free land → ikrar to a **registered Nazhir** before the **PPAIW (Kepala KUA or Notaris-PPAIW)** with 2 witnesses in a Majelis Ikrar Wakaf → AIW → PPAIW registers at **BPN**, which issues the Sertipikat Tanah Wakaf **in the Nazhir's name**. Perpetual, irrevocable, cannot be sold/inherited (UU 41/2004, PP 42/2006 as amended by PP 25/2018, PMA 73/2013, Permen ATR 2/2017, PerBWI 3/2008).
- A non-Nazhir platform/yayasan may facilitate (intake, documents, scheduling) but no regulation gives it a role: it must not receive land, sign/issue the AIW or certificate, or collect "wakaf" money (that must go to the Nazhir / LKS-PWU account). YIEM could register as Nazhir badan hukum if its AD fits.
- Wakaf keluarga = wakaf ahli (PP 42 Art. 30): benefits blood relatives, beneficiaries attend the ikrar, becomes wakaf khairi when the line dies out; wakaf sosial = khairi.
- Wakaf law mandates productive management (Nazhir cut ≤10%), but PP 9/1987 Art. 6(3) (in force) forbids commercial management of non-public cemeteries; selling plots on wakaf land is criminal (UU 41 Arts. 40, 67).
- Digital: steps 1-2, status tracking, KUA's own e-AIW/SIWAK. In person/official: survey (PPAIW check), majelis ikrar + AIW, BPN certification. Move "Penilaian/peruntukan" before the akad.
