# Manual payments, direct payment to the Lokasi and Harga Khusus

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 25
Spec: Domain modules > 10. Billing (Payment: manual, direct, Rp 0); 11. Payouts (Harga Khusus, Dibayar langsung); stories 134, 163, 164

## What to build

The payment paths outside the provider. Admin Platform marks a Tagihan paid by hand (Transfer manual / Tunai) with an uploaded proof. The Admin Lokasi records "Dibayar langsung ke Lokasi Mitra" with proof when a family paid the Lokasi directly; Admin Platform can reverse it. Admin Platform sets a Harga Khusus on an order, which cancels and reissues the Tagihan with a negative "Penyesuaian Harga Khusus" line; a resulting Rp 0 Tagihan is Lunas at once. Each path issues exactly one Bukti Pembayaran and runs the same downstream effects as a provider payment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Manual payment requires a proof file; the Bukti Pembayaran shows method Transfer manual or Tunai; audited.
- [ ] Direct payment: the Bukti Pembayaran reads "diterima oleh Lokasi Mitra X"; it records that no tariff Pencairan is due and a platform-fee Potongan is owed (consumed by ticket 32); reversible by Admin Platform only, audited.
- [ ] Harga Khusus: Admin Platform only, with a reason; the Tagihan is reissued (never edited) with a negative line. Admin Platform may enter on the order the partner share (the amount the Lokasi Mitra agreed to bear) with a required note; default 0, in which case the Operator bears the whole reduction (from the Biaya Layanan Platform first, then its own funds). A non-zero partner share lowers that order's Pencairan (ticket 32); audited.
- [ ] A Rp 0 Tagihan becomes Lunas immediately with method "Tanpa pembayaran (Harga Khusus)".
- [ ] Each path fires the downstream-effect registry once (e.g. Bukti Pemesanan issued).
- [ ] Tests: each method's Bukti Pembayaran wording; direct-payment reversal; Harga Khusus reissue and Rp 0 waiver; partner share requires a note and defaults to 0; immutability preserved.

## Comments

- 2026-09-26 — From ticket 18 review (user decision): a reissued Tagihan keeps the original due date. So Admin Platform cannot reissue (e.g. add a Harga Khusus to) a pay-first Tagihan that is already past its due date: the action is refused with a clear message, and the order is placed again instead. A Rp 0 Tagihan is already Lunas at issue (ticket 18).

- 2026-09-27 — Builder report (ticket 30). Migration `drizzle/0025_breezy_mantis.sql`: 8 nullable `ADD COLUMN`s (`bukti_pembayaran.proof_key`, and `partner_share*` / `bayar_langsung*` on `pemesanan_makam`) plus one `CHECK`. Pure expand, no `-- contract:` needed, no trigger touched. The orchestrator renumbers if 32 takes 0025.
- 2026-09-27 — **Conflict, not resolved here: the reversal of a "dibayar langsung" payment is ticket 31 and is not built.** "Reversible by Admin Platform only" means cancelling a numbered Bukti Pembayaran whose link may already have gone to the family, the Bukti Pemesanan, the Hak Pakai and the effects already fired — that is refund machinery, and building half of it here would be duplicated by 31. What this ticket does instead: the recording is the Lokasi Mitra's own Admin Lokasi only (`pembayaran.catat_langsung`), written once and never editable, and no function anywhere undoes it. Ticket 31 needs from here: a public way to read the record (`pemesanan.pembayaranOrder(nomor)` → `pembayaran.kind === "langsung_ke_lokasi_mitra"` with its `pada` and `bukti`), the proof itself (Billing's `urlBukti`, Admin Platform only), and a public way to clear the record on the order when it reverses. AC 6's "direct-payment reversal" test is therefore **not** in this branch: what is here is that no one but Admin Platform may reverse it, and nobody may do it at all yet.
- 2026-09-27 — **Conflict, not resolved here: the partner-share freeze after a Pencairan has been issued cannot be enforced yet, because Payouts does not exist (ticket 32).** The rule's input is Payouts' own state, and `src/domain/payouts/index.ts` is still the walking skeleton's placeholder with no tables and no public read. The rule is written against a named seam instead of a predicate that lies: `PemesananDeps.pencairanTerbit(nomorPemesanan)` (optional; the composition root supplies nothing, so today no order has been paid out and the guard cannot bind), the refusal `partner_share_sudah_terkunci` is there, and the test drives it through a stand-in read. What *is* enforced now: a share is only ever written on an order whose Tagihan can still be reissued, so no share is entered on an order that has already been paid. Ticket 32 wires one line and nothing else changes. Two sub-decisions I had to make and flag: a later Harga Khusus with the share field left empty **keeps** the share that was agreed (a share is not withdrawn by leaving a field empty; a note without a share is refused as `input_tidak_valid`), and a share may not exceed the reduction it shares.
- 2026-09-27 — **Both money facts live on the order, in the module that owns it, read by Payouts through one public projection.** `partnerShare` on `pemesanan_makam` (as spec:503, AC 30, AC 32, `00-index.md` #12 and CONTEXT.md all say: a Tagihan is *replaced* by a Harga Khusus, so a share kept there would be lost by its own reissue), and the direct-payment fact beside it, because a fact Payouts cannot read would leave the order owing a full tariff Pencairan *and* a Potongan — paying the Lokasi Mitra twice for work that never received money. `pemesanan.pembayaranOrder(nomorPemesanan)` is the one read, and it states the consequence rather than leaving it to be derived again on the other side: `pembayaran.kind === "langsung_ke_lokasi_mitra"` carries `biayaPlatform` (the Biaya Layanan Platform on that order's Tagihan, which is the Potongan owed) and means no tariff is due.
- 2026-09-27 — **Scope note: the direct-payment fact has a home only on `pemesanan_makam`, because a Pemesanan Makam is the only order kind with a Tagihan today** (a Terencana order is issued its Tagihan at its confirmation, ticket 37; Perpanjangan and Layanan orders do not exist yet). When those order kinds land, each of their modules needs the same two columns on its own order plus a projection of the same shape, or Payouts will meet a Perpanjangan with a Harga Khusus and nowhere to read it. Its own module, not this one, is the right place to add it.
- 2026-09-27 — **Conflict, not resolved here: an Admin Lokasi cannot read the direct-payment proof back, and cannot reverse it either.** Reading it needs Billing to know which Lokasi Mitra an order belongs to, which it does not (it holds `nomor_pemesanan` and `place_name` as text, with no link to an order — the same shape the Atrean's Tier 2 Pembayaran Perlu Ditinjau row has no page for). Today the Admin Lokasi is the one who uploaded it, and Admin Platform (who reverses) reads it through `billing.urlBukti`. A per-order-kind read in Billing, or a proof read on the order, is the fix and belongs with ticket 31's reversal screen.
- 2026-09-27 — **The reissue guard that was missing is in Billing, not in the caller, and its test was red before the fix:** `REISSUABLE` accepts `belum_dibayar`, and nothing checked `kind === "pay_first" && dueAt <= now`, so because the lapse tick has not run a past-due pay-first Tagihan could still be replaced — handing a family a fresh Tagihan that is already past the due date it keeps. `reissueTagihan` now refuses it with `batas_pembayaran_lewat`, the same rule a payment is held to, and the order is placed again instead (the 2026-09-26 decision above). `src/domain/billing/tagihan.test.ts` shows the reissue succeeding on the old code.
- 2026-09-27 — **No menu item and no list: the new pages are reachable by URL only.** `/staf/admin-platform/tagihan/[tagihanId]` (a Tagihan, its lines, the manual payment with proof, the Harga Khusus, the Bukti) and, on the Admin Lokasi's own order page, the direct-payment form. What points at the Tagihan page from the product is the Antrean's overdue-Tagihan rows and the chasing list, which are not built (ticket 29), so no menu href exists to fail `pageExists`; adding one would mean inventing a Tagihan list this ticket was not asked for. The `tagihanId` is a uuid, so the page is not reachable from a Nomor Tagihan typed by hand either — that is the search entry point ticket 29 should bring.
- 2026-09-27 — Two choices worth a reviewer's eye, both made because the spec is silent: a second Harga Khusus on one order is **allowed** (each adds its own Penyesuaian line to the replacement, since a Tagihan is never edited) rather than refused, and a manual payment on a Tagihan that is Lunas already is **refused** (`pembayaran_sudah_ada`) instead of returning the existing Bukti, so a slip is never stored for a payment that did not happen and the second click of a double submit says so. The provider path keeps its "return the same Bukti" behaviour; `recordPayment` now also reports `settled` so a caller can tell the two apart.
