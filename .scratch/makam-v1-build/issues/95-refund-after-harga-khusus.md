# Refunds of a Keluhan and a Layanan cancellation after a Harga Khusus are refused

Status: resolved
Blocked by: —
Spec: spec.md, Billing (Harga Khusus, refunds) and Layanan (Keluhan outcome, cancellation)

## What to build

After a Harga Khusus, the Tagihan's total is reduced by one negative Penyesuaian line but the tariff lines are not, so a refund of a whole line (`layanan/keluhan.ts` `mintaPengembalian`, `layanan/batal.ts`) is sent to Refunds' `ajukanBaris` and refused as `melebihi_tagihan` (`sudah + jumlah > tagihan.total`); a Keluhan then reads `pengembalian_tidak_bisa_diajukan`. Ticket 38's Pembatalan Terencana avoids it by apportioning the Penyesuaian per line before computing the refund (`floor(line × (jumlahHarga + penyesuaian) / jumlahHarga)`, `pemesanan/pembatalan-terencana.ts`). Owner decision 2026-09-30: use the same per-line proportional rule for every refund of a Harga Khusus Tagihan, in one place (Refunds or Billing), not copied per caller. The Biaya Layanan Platform line is handled by the same rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Keluhan whose outcome is a refund, and a Layanan cancellation refund, after a Harga Khusus refund the line's proportional share of the reduced total; the sum of all refunds of a Tagihan never exceeds what was paid.
- [ ] Ticket 38's Pembatalan uses the same shared rule (its own apportionment code is removed, its tests still pass).
- [ ] Tests in glossary terms through public functions: a Harga Khusus then a Keluhan refund, a Layanan cancellation refund, two refunds on one Tagihan, and rounding down never over-refunds.

## Spec gaps and decisions for the owner

- 2026-10-01 — **FEE DOUBLE-HAIRCUT (money, NOT decided here).** Owner decision 2026-09-30 says the Biaya Layanan Platform is "handled by the same rule", so `refunds/request.ts:285` apportions the fee with the tariff-only rule too. Consequence: the fee absorbs `F·|P|/T`, on top of the tariff absorbing `|P|`, so the family is under-refunded by `F·|P|/T` beyond the Harga Khusus. Numbers: (1) tariff Rp 750.000 + fee Rp 150.000, Harga Khusus Rp 50.000 → family paid Rp 850.000, refund totals 700.000 + 140.000 = Rp 840.000, **Rp 10.000 short**; (2) two lines Rp 750.000 each + fee Rp 150.000, Harga Khusus Rp 50.000 → paid Rp 1.600.000, refund 725.000 + 145.000 + 725.000 = Rp 1.595.000, **Rp 5.000 short**. Always ≤ paid (AC holds), but short of it. The owner should choose: (i) keep as-is (always ≤, slightly under), (ii) refund the fee at face value `F` so tariff `(T−|P|)` + fee `F` = total exactly, or (iii) spread the Penyesuaian over the fee too (`(T+F)` base) so every line is reduced proportionally and the sums equal the total. Reported, not implemented. **Diputuskan pemilik 2026-10-01 (ditetapkan, bukan varian di atas): Harga Khusus ditanggung dari biaya dulu; tarif penuh sampai biaya habis, kelebihannya proporsional; refund = tarif + biaya terbayar = tepat yang dibayar. Lihat entri keputusan dan builder di atas.**
- 2026-10-01 — **Clamp fallback (judgement, implemented).** With `|P| > T` the tariff-only rule is undefined (the Penyesuaian exceeds the tariff). A paid line now returns its proportional share of the total actually paid (`floor(line × total / Σ positive lines)`), min 1 rupiah, instead of 0/negative. The owner may prefer a different fallback (e.g. the fee-first allocation the spec names for who bears the Harga Khusus), but the current choice keeps AC1 (sum ≤ paid) and the "never refused" requirement.
